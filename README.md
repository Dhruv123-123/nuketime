# nuketime

Research on company ideas in energy, nuclear, data centers, SMRs, and construction.

- `RESEARCH.md`: ranked, stress-tested ideas with evidence, economics, competition, kill criteria, and a survival matrix.
- `MOONSHOTS.md`: seven YC-scale moonshots for a world-class technical team, with the hard technical bet, wedge, and ten-billion path for each.
- `SOFTWARE_MOONSHOTS.md`: nine moonshots attacked through software or one clever hardware component rather than construction: the nuclear software stack, the safety chip, the reactor-agnostic back half, synthetic firm power, rack-to-grid smoothing, dynamic rating twins, automated defect recognition.
- `PRINCIPAL_MOONSHOTS.md`: seven positions where the company owns the asset (plants, electrons, compute, fuel, spent fuel, orbit, heat) rather than selling to someone who does.
- `research/`: the five sourced briefings the ranking is built on (SMR landscape, data-center power, construction, startup landscape, policy and economics).
- `designs/rocksink/`: a complete soft design, ROCKSINK, that rejects the decay heat of a shaft-sited 250 MWth SMR module into bedrock through 128 gas-loaded gravity thermosyphons in fanned upholes, with a validated transient conduction model, sizing, sensitivity, figures and a design report (`DESIGN.md`).
- `designs/cavern/`: a second complete soft design, CAVERN, that stores a PWR's midday steam as pressurised hot water in two lined rock caverns and returns it as 315 MW of evening peak output at a 74.6 % electric round trip, with steam-table thermodynamics, rock conduction, uplift and heave checks, an economic scan, figures and a design report (`DESIGN.md`).
- `designs/lockstep/`: a third design, LOCKSTEP, that engineers a PWR and a co-located data centre as one island: grid-loss ride-through on the data-centre load with no reactor trip (simulated with a reduced-order plant model), shared cooling-tower heat rejection (Merkel model over an hourly year), and the data centre's backup fleet as a diverse AC source for the safety buses (blackout-risk model), with figures and a design report (`DESIGN.md`).
- `research/NUCLEAR_VERTICAL_ML_MAP.md`: the whole nuclear vertical in 59 steps, from regional targeting to decommissioning and safeguards, each with what happens today, the bottleneck, the data, who is already applying software or ML, the gap, the prize and a verdict; six theses; twenty ranked opportunities; a path for a prospecting-ML company. Generated from `research/vertical_map_data.py` by `research/build_map.py`; sources in `research/vertical_map_sources.md`.
- `research/ANALOG_ATLAS.md`: the Analogue Atlas. For each of the twenty ranked opportunities in the vertical map, the industry that already solved the structurally identical problem, the method and measured gain (tagged independent, vendor or synthetic), the regulatory document that let a model or process be credited, the nuclear rule that could carry the transfer today, what maps and what breaks, and who to hire; ten transfer patterns, a scorecard graded direct, adapt or partial, and a hiring map. Generated from `research/analog_atlas_data.py` by `research/build_atlas.py`.
- `research/converge/PICK.md`: the convergence memo. Scores every candidate in this repo against a team that already does ML uranium prospecting, picks one company (find the uranium US ISR plants can actually produce, starting with pounds their historic logs missed), and gives the brief, the competition, and an eight-week validation plan with named calls and kill criteria. Built on two sourced briefings in the same folder (`exploration_market.md`, `isr_wedges.md`).
- `designs/wellfield/`: WELLFIELD, a reduced-order ISR uranium wellfield and plant model used to test whether closed-loop wellfield operation is worth a company. Over 24 simulated plants it is worth about 3 % NPV, and a higher shut-in grade alone is worth about 5 %, so the pick moved upstream (`DESIGN.md`).
- `research/converge/PICK2.md`: the second convergence memo, written after prospecting and drilling ideas were ruled out. Rescores everything and picks a 100 % X-ray inspection and sorting station for TRISO fuel particles, sold to the new US coated-particle fuel lines; with the prototype results, brief, risks and an eight-week validation plan with named calls and kill criteria. Sourced notes beside it (`fuel_cycle_notes.md`, `fleet_ops_notes.md`, `overlooked_notes.md`, `lost_mw_notes.md`).
- `designs/release/`: RELEASE, the test of that pick. A lot-release model (destructive burn-leach sampling against 100 % inspection), a voxel X-ray model of a TRISO particle that measures which SiC defects radiography can catch at line rate (missing and thin SiC yes, tight cracks no), and the re-costed case for a sorter in front of today's release test, worth about $1M to $30M a year per 5 MTU line (`DESIGN.md`).
- `designs/megawatts/`: MEGAWATTS, a reduced-order PWR secondary-cycle model with drifting instruments and degrading equipment, used to test lost-megawatt monitoring as a company. Better detection is worth about 1 MWe per unit over conventional KPIs, against installed incumbents, so it was not picked (`DESIGN.md`).
- `research/converge/PICK3.md`: the third convergence memo, written after the TRISO sorter was judged too niche, with size as the first filter. Picks a nuclear-qualified contract manufacturer (Hadrian for nuclear, with software quality records as the moat), scores eight big-ceiling candidates, and gives the brief, risks and an eight-week validation plan. Sourced notes beside it (`big_nuclear_notes.md`, `big_energy_notes.md`).
- `designs/nshop/`: NSHOP, a queueing model of US nuclear-qualified manufacturing capacity against three reactor build scenarios to 2036, showing when lead times explode and what qualifying capacity faster is worth to the projects (`DESIGN.md`).
- `research/converge/PICK4.md`: the fourth hunt, across everything in nuclear (about 70 agents, about 330 hypotheses in three rounds). No new idea cleared both novelty and size; it states the law that fell out (novel gaps are small, big gaps are named), how each closest call died (hafnium recovery, Zr-91-stripped cladding, neutron soft errors in liquid-cooled AI racks), the negative results, and recommends committing to the third pick. Raw agent output in `gap_hunt_r1.md` to `gap_hunt_r3.md`.
- `designs/zirc/`: ZIRC, the two zirconium models that killed the closest calls (`DESIGN.md`).
