# nuketime

Research on company ideas in energy, nuclear, data centers, SMRs, and construction.

- `RESEARCH.md`: ranked, stress-tested ideas with evidence, economics, competition, kill criteria, and a survival matrix.
- `MOONSHOTS.md`: seven YC-scale moonshots for a world-class technical team, with the hard technical bet, wedge, and ten-billion path for each.
- `SOFTWARE_MOONSHOTS.md`: nine moonshots attacked through software or one clever hardware component rather than construction: the nuclear software stack, the safety chip, the reactor-agnostic back half, synthetic firm power, rack-to-grid smoothing, dynamic rating twins, automated defect recognition.
- `PRINCIPAL_MOONSHOTS.md`: seven positions where the company owns the asset (plants, electrons, compute, fuel, spent fuel, orbit, heat) rather than selling to someone who does.
- `research/`: the five sourced briefings the ranking is built on (SMR landscape, data-center power, construction, startup landscape, policy and economics).
- `designs/rocksink/`: a complete soft design, ROCKSINK, that rejects the decay heat of a shaft-sited 250 MWth SMR module into bedrock through 128 gas-loaded gravity thermosyphons in fanned upholes, with a validated transient conduction model, sizing, sensitivity, figures and a design report (`DESIGN.md`).
- `designs/cavern/`: a second complete soft design, CAVERN, that stores a PWR's midday steam as pressurised hot water in two lined rock caverns and returns it as 315 MW of evening peak output at a 74.6 % electric round trip, with steam-table thermodynamics, rock conduction, uplift and heave checks, an economic scan, figures and a design report (`DESIGN.md`).
