## Durable vs fragile tailwinds
Durable: 45Y/48E to 2033 + 45U + transferability; Price-Anderson, Part 53, GEIS; load growth + turbine scarcity + PJM cap; state money/law; Army Janus; international.
Fragile: EDF §1706 sunset Sept 30, 2028; DOE-authorized pilots have no commercial path without NRC; NRC independence erosion; 202(c) and FERC large-load jurisdiction; tariffs and rates; hyperscaler PPA appetite contingent on AI monetization.
