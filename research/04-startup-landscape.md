## 8. Crowded vs thin
Crowded: new reactor designs/microreactors; AP1000 fleet developers; bridging gas (VoltaGrid); hyperscale powered-campus development; nuclear document-search/licensing genAI; SSTs; fusion/geothermal developers; developer-side interconnection analytics.
Thin: (1) large-load interconnection process tooling for utilities/ISOs; (2) nuclear construction execution (schedule, QA/QC, N-stamp doc control, modular yard workflows); (3) uprate/restart engineering services; (4) GET pilot-to-deployment risk transfer; (5) fuel-cycle picks and shovels (transport, deconversion, waste, qualification data); (6) pipeline/air/water permitting for on-site generation; (7) turbine/transformer secondary markets and refurbishment; (8) training data/simulators for plants and DCs.
