# R6: Overlooked practical niches in nuclear/energy (research notes, 2026-10-03)

## Topic 1: Nuclear + data center co-location interface
- Talen-Amazon restructured June 11, 2025 from behind-the-meter to front-of-meter 1,920 MW PPA (through 2042) after FERC rejected the expanded ISA (Nov 2024). https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1622536/000162828025030559/a20250611pressreleasebusin.htm ; https://www.theregister.com/2025/06/12/amazon_talen_nuclear_deal/
- FERC Dec 18, 2025 order: PJM must create 3 new transmission services for co-located load (interim non-firm, firm contract demand, non-firm contract demand); 60 days paper hearing. https://www.utilitydive.com/news/ferc-pjm-colocation-data-center/808368/
- NERC incident review (Jan 8, 2025): July 10, 2024, ~1,500 MW data-center load dropped after a 230 kV fault in Eastern Interconnection; 1,260 MW sustained. Cause: DC protection counting voltage disturbances interacting with auto-reclosing -> transfer to UPS/backup. https://www.nerc.com/globalassets/our-work/reports/event-reports/incident_review_large_load_loss.pdf
- NERC Level 3 alert May 5, 2026 (rare): 7 essential actions for TPs/TOPs/BAs on "computational loads" - modeling data, commissioning processes, dynamic fault recorders, min/max MW data. Response due Aug 3, 2026. Entities "generally did not have sufficient processes". https://www.utilitydive.com/news/nerc-issues-rare-level-3-alert-over-data-center-load-losses/819295/
- ERCOT published PSCAD AI data-center dynamic model manual Apr 24, 2026. https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/04/24/Dynamic-Modeling-of-AI-Data-Center-Load-in-PSCAD-Manual.pdf
- Assessment: the nuclear-specific interface (load rejection -> turbine runback/reactor trip, offsite power GDC-17) is real but buyers are ~5 utilities + hyperscalers; most deals moved front-of-meter, so physically co-located nuclear+DC is rare. The broader "large load modeling/commissioning/fault recording" market is driven by NERC L3 but sits close to excluded generic DC-grid. Incumbents: engineering consultancies (Keentel, EPE, TRC), PSCAD/Siemens PTI, SEL relays/recorders. Strongest argument against: consulting-shaped, few buyers, standard work done by existing EPCs. VERDICT: weak for a startup unless framed as large-load EMT model validation-as-a-service.

## Topic 2: Medical isotopes (Mo-99, Lu-177, Ac-225)
- Pluvicto FY2025 net sales $1,994M (+43%), Q4 2025 $605M (+72%). https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/radioligand-therapy-manufacturing-capacity (citing Novartis)
- Lutathera+Pluvicto $1.7B in H1 2026; treatment centers 86 (Nov 2022) -> 700+; bottleneck shifted since 2024 from manufacturing to treatment capacity: nuc-med chairs, radiopharmacy staffing, reimbursement; patient waits 2-6 weeks. https://www.biospace.com/business/3-steps-for-taking-on-novartis-in-radiopharma-curium-up-first (2026)
- Curium-Lantheus up to $8.0B (Aug 2026); ~$7B bid reported May 22, 2026. https://deepceutix.com/insights/radioligand-isotope-logistics
- Lu-177 t1/2 6.647 d; Pluvicto shelf life 120 h; ~10%/day potency loss; Novartis runs a 30-40 person team scheduling doses "to the minute". Same deepceutix source.
- Ac-225 legacy supply ~1.7 Ci/yr across 4 sites, 100-200 patients/yr (intuitionlabs citing DOE); ~2 Ci 2024 (https://www.eos-intelligence.com/implicium/health-care/ac-225-supply-shortage-forces-pharma-to-lock-in-cancer-isotope-deals/). NorthStar routine commercial-scale Ac-225 via e-accelerator Jan 6, 2026. Cardinal expanded Ac-225 capacity Apr 1, 2026 (https://newsroom.cardinalhealth.com/2026-04-01-Cardinal-Health-expands-Actinium-225-production-capacity-to-support-growing-demand-for-novel-cancer-therapies). SHINE $263M conditional DOE loan Apr 2026. ARTBIO $132M Series B Jul 2025.
- Mo-99: recurring reactor outages: HFR pipe deformation Oct 2024 -> up to 40% shortages; US ~40,000 Tc-99m scans/day. https://www.auntminnie.com/clinical-news/molecular-imaging/article/15706252/tc99m-shortage-expected-into-november (Oct 18, 2024). BR2 Oct 2022. NorthStar exited Mo-99 (93 jobs) 2023.
- Clinic ops pain: UCSD/Herbert Wertheim (Sep 15, 2026) "manual coordination will not hold up" - scheduling labs, pre-infusion review, drug availability, room/staff, post-imaging and cycles; only ~10 RPT cases/week; deploying GE iRT for Theranostics. https://research.gehealthcare.com/across-the-enterprise/were-reaching-a-point-where-manual-coordination-will-not-hold-up-dr-ranjini-tolakanahalli-talks-about-why-the-herbert-wertheim-cancer-institute-is-deploying/
- Competitors in clinic software: GE iRT, Siemens ARIA CORE for Theranostics, DOSIsoft Theranostics (new entity May 26, 2026), Nuctrac, MIM (GE). Isotope producers are capital-heavy (SHINE >$1B raised).
- Assessment: Real money and real pain, but (a) producer side is capex/hardware heavy, (b) clinic-workflow side now has GE/Siemens/DOSIsoft moving in during 2026. Overlooked gap: the *middle* - radiopharmacy/CDMO production + dose allocation across decaying inventory (batch-to-patient matching, reschedules, waste) for the 20+ new radioligand sponsors that lack Novartis's 30-40 person team. Strongest argument against: buyers are pharma with long validation cycles (GxP, 21 CFR Part 11); World Courier/Cardinal/Cencora bundle logistics; "nuclear" angle is thin.

## Topic 3: Rad-tolerant electronics and sensors
- Suppliers concentrated: Mirion (R981/R985/RC720 rad-tolerant cameras), Diakont, Rolls-Royce, Ahlberg, Nucron, Thermo Fisher CIDTEC. Tube cameras ~1 MGy lifetime; CCD/CMOS 10^6-10^7 rad; CCD modules replaced every 3-5 yrs. https://www.neimagazine.com/advanced-reactorsfusion/eyes-inside-radiation-tolerant-cctv/ (updated Feb 23, 2024)
- MAGICS (Belgium) + SCK CEN: rad-hard camera with CZT gamma spectroscopy for dismantling, Jul 3, 2025. https://www.magics.tech/news/radiation-resistant-camera
- NASA FSP directive Aug 2025: 100 kWe lunar reactor, launch-ready Q1 FY2030, up to 2 awards within 6 months of RFP. https://www.neimagazine.com/news/nasa-fast-tracks-lunar-reactor-plan/
- DOE ASI program (INL) funds in-core/high-temp sensors (e.g., 1,000C microsensors, Oct 2025 https://techxplore.com/news/2025-10-sensors-generation-nuclear-reactors.html). 
- Assessment: genuine niche but small, slow, hardware-qualification heavy (rad testing, ASME/IEEE 323/344 EQ), dominated by Mirion + national labs. Buyers buy in tiny volumes; space nuclear is 1-2 primes. Strongest argument against: no 12-month shippable product for an ML-strong team; TAM tens of $M. VERDICT: skip, except as a component of a robotics/inspection play.

## Topic 4: Nuclear-grade manufacturing inspection / NDE / quality
- US-Westinghouse $80B AP1000 partnership, Oct 28, 2025. https://neutronbytes.com/2025/10/28/feds-in-80-billion-partnership-with-westinghouse-to-build-ap1000s/
- $17.5B conditional DOE loan commitment (Jun 24, 2026) for AP1000 long-lead items: heavy forgings/castings for pressure vessels, turbines, generators, components and control software; "a limited number of heavy engineering plants" can make them; goal 10 large reactors under construction by 2030; up to 3 years schedule acceleration. https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/us-federal-loan-to-jumpstart-ap1000-reactor-supply-chain
- Gecko Robotics + BPMI (Naval Nuclear Propulsion), Jul 15, 2025: AI/robotic inspection of forgings cut inspection from weeks to days (up to 90%). https://www.geckorobotics.com/news/gecko-robotics-bpmi . Gecko valued ~$1.25B; $71M Navy deal. Path Robotics deal with largest US military shipbuilder. => incumbents already in nuclear-adjacent forging/weld NDE.
- NDT workforce: ~30% of certified NDT personnel over 55 (ASNT 2023). https://www.qualitymag.com/articles/98711-behind-the-scenes-behind-schedule-ndts-workforce-shortage
- ML for UT NDE: ORNL/PNNL/NRC assessments (NRC ML22284A071) - regulators studying but no code acceptance yet; ASME Section V/XI performance demonstration (PDI) gates any ML; TrueFlaw (Finland) sells AI for NDT + flawed training samples. https://trueflaw.com/ml
- Knowledge/QA records: ANS Sep 1, 2026 "Nuclear's next chapter has a knowledge challenge" - retirements; QA records (Appendix B / NQA-1) as only answer. https://www.ans.org/news/2026-09-01/article-8321/nuclears-next-chapter-has-a-knowledge-challenge/
- Nuclearn $10.5M Series A Sep 10, 2025 (AI for plant ops). https://www.ans.org/news/2025-09-10/article-7356/ai-startup-nuclearn-nets-105m-in-series-a-funding/ ; NucFinder supplier discovery exists.
- CFSI: NRC OIG reports on counterfeit/fraudulent/suspect items at plants (https://www.oversight.gov/report/NRC/Audit-Nuclear-Regulatory-Commissions-Oversight-Counterfeit-Fraudulent-and-Suspect-Items).
- Assessment: Raw NDE robotics = Gecko/Path/Eddyfi/Zetec/Westinghouse WesDyne own it. The overlooked wedge: supplier-side software for *new entrants to nuclear manufacturing* (machine shops trying to get N-stamp/NQA-1, and commercial-grade dedication): automated CMTR verification against ASME SA/SFA specs, traveler/NCR/data-package generation (N-1/NPV-1 data reports), CFSI flags. Buyers: hundreds of shops entering via AP1000/SMR + utilities doing CGD. Strongest argument against: QA culture favors paper/consultants; validated software itself must be under NQA-1 (software QA, Subpart 2.7); small ACVs per shop.

## Topic 5: Decommissioning and waste characterization
- Hanford FY26 record $3.2B (Jan 18, 2026); WA Ecology says $6.15B needed to meet legal obligations; LAW vitrification plant began operating Oct 2025, got $480M; tank-waste targets 2043-2052 (DOE internally 2069). https://www.opb.org/article/2026/01/18/hanford-nuclear-cleanup-gets-federal-budget-bump/
- UK NDA 2025/26 planned spend £4.164B (Sellafield £2.805B, ~67%); £4.7M to 5 orgs for autonomous sort & segregate, on-site testing mid-2027; £2M remote sensing. https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/nuclear-decommissioning-authority-business-plan-2025-to-2028/nuclear-decommissioning-authority-business-plan-2025-to-2028
- Sellafield: Spot robot (Createc) doing swab/smear sampling now business-as-usual (2026); cleanup £136B to 2125. https://www.autonocion.com/us/sellafield-robot-dog-nuclear/
- SRNL AI for cleanup (Genesis Mission), claim >$150B lifecycle savings (Aug 18, 2026). https://interestingengineering.com/science/ai-nuclear-cleanup-150-billion-savings
- US commercial decommissioning pipeline is *shrinking*: Palisades fuel load began Aug 31, 2026 (https://www.ans.org/news/2026-08-31/article-8355/fuel-loading-process-begins-at-palisades/); Crane (TMI-1) restart; Duane Arnold restart target 2029 (https://www.utilitydive.com/news/what-does-it-take-restore-nuclear-power-plant/820831/, Jun 2, 2026). Holtec/EnergySolutions/NorthStar own commercial D&D.
- Assessment: Money is huge (>$11B/yr combined US EM ~$8B+ and NDA £4B) but procurement is via M&O prime contractors (Bechtel, Amentum, BWXT, Jacobs) and UK Tier-1s; sales cycles 2-5 yrs; innovation funds are £M-scale grants. Overlooked wedge: waste characterisation data -> waste-acceptance-criteria paperwork (gamma spec + drum assay + process knowledge -> certification packages for WIPP/LLW). Strongest argument against: government-only customers, slow, ITAR/security clearances, decommissioning revenue lumpy; restarts shrink commercial pipeline.

## Topic 6: Other publicly named bottlenecks (2025-2026)
### 6a. Uprates / "find the lost megawatts"
- DOE UPRISE launched Mar 12, 2026: 2.5 GW by 2027, 5 GW by 2029 via uprates/restarts. DOE DAS Rian Bahran named bottlenecks: "engineering schedule and bandwidth at utilities", long-lead equipment, staffing. NEI 2025 survey: 73% of sites interested, >8 GW potential this decade. https://www.powermag.com/doe-unveils-initiative-to-add-5-gw-of-nuclear-capacity-through-uprates-and-restarts/
- NRC expected uprate applications (page updated Sep 8, 2026): 31 applications, 2,421 MWe, 2026-2032; 2027 peak with 17 apps; 10 MUR, 2 stretch, 19 EPU. https://www.nrc.gov/reactors/operating/licensing/power-uprates/status-power-apps/expected-applications
- Vistra-Meta Jan 9, 2026: 20-yr PPAs, 2,609 MW incl 433 MW of uprates at Perry, Davis-Besse, Beaver Valley through 2034. https://investor.vistracorp.com/2026-01-09-Vistra-and-Meta-Announce-Agreements-to-Support-Nuclear-Plants-in-PJM-and-Add-New-Nuclear-Generation-to-the-Grid  => hyperscalers now pay for uprate MW.
- INL review (Jul 24, 2026): above ~20% uprates become a "balance-of-plant throughput problem" (steam path choking, condenser backpressure, FW pump/valve margins). https://inl.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/a-review-of-limitations-upgrade-strategies-and-performance-projec/
- INL LWRS: 172 uprates done (~8 reactors' worth); ~16,500 MWth remaining potential; MUR up to 2%, SPU 2-7%, EPU up to 20%. https://lwrs.inl.gov/content/uploads/11/2024/07/UprateExistingNuclearReactors.pdf
- Data Validation & Reconciliation (VDI 2048): Bruce Power recovered 42 MW across 6 of 8 units (ANS, Jan 29, 2026). https://www.ans.org/news/2026-01-29/article-7717/power-uprates-curtisswright-discusses-reconciled-data/
- NEI 2025 cost: $36.46/MWh total; labor ~$8.8B >half of opex; CF 92%. https://www.nei.org/getContentAsset/47fa8caa-9b0d-4029-932c-07f902e82f4f/8d8ff8d6-b2ae-401b-a63c-f6b108e809d2/2025-Costs-in-Context-1.pdf
- Incumbents: Curtiss-Wright/Scientech PEPSE + DVR, Belsim VALI, BTB-Jansky, GSE (EtaPRO), Sargent & Lundy, Framatome, Westinghouse. Nuclearn ($10.5M A) adjacent.
- Value math (my calc): 1 MWe at ~$80/MWh hyperscaler PPA x 8,760 h x 0.92 = ~$0.64M/yr; Bruce's 42 MW ~ $20-27M/yr.
- Assessment: strongest software-native wedge found. ML team can build physics-informed state estimation (DVR) + heat-balance surrogate for uprate scoping and continuous MW recovery. Against: DVR for MUR licensing in US needs NRC acceptance (topical report) - slow; conservative buyers; Curtiss-Wright already markets it; ~54 sites = small logo count (but high ACV).

### 6b. Physical security modernization / drones
- ~26 drone overflights of US reactors in 2024; ~100 civilian plants (19% of US electricity) lack counter-UAS authority. https://thebulletin.org/2025/09/its-past-time-to-start-protecting-us-nuclear-power-reactors-from-drones/ (Sep 2025)
- FY2026 NDAA Sec. 3111 gives DOE C-UAS at its nuclear facilities; SAFER SKIES lets DHS/DOJ authorize SLTT ops after rules. https://dronelife.com/2025/12/09/ndaa-fy-2026-key-counter-uas-provisions-explained/
- NRC "Modernizing Security Requirements" proposed rule Jun 26, 2026: prescriptive -> performance-based, "technology-inclusive", savings $561M-$1.01B/30 yrs; comments closed Jul 27, 2026. https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/06/26/2026-12989/modernizing-security-requirements
- NDA £2M for autonomous security monitoring at lower-risk sites (BP 2025-28).
- Assessment: Security is a big chunk of the ~$8.8B labor; performance-based rule could let sensors/autonomy substitute for guard posts, esp. for microreactors that can't afford 100+ officers. Against: rule not final; detection is commoditized (Dedrone/Axon, DroneShield); mitigation still legally restricted for commercial plants; security vendors must be trusted (clearances).
- UPDATE on DVR regulatory path: EPRI Topical Report 3002018337 is NRC-approved; PSEG Salem LAR (Feb 6, 2026) asks to make DVR the primary MUR method (replacing Crossflow UFM), vendor GSE Performance Solutions + Belsim. https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML2603/ML26037A270.pdf . BTB Jansky met NRC May 15, 2025 on DVR/PDR power recovery. https://www.nrc.gov/public-involve/public-meetings/pmns/20250584
- => Path is de-risked by EPRI TR, but at least 4 vendors (GSE/Belsim, Curtiss-Wright, BTB Jansky, Westinghouse) are active. A startup must differentiate on speed of deployment, ML diagnostics of WHICH component is losing MW, and selling outcome-based (share of recovered MWh) to merchant owners (Constellation, Vistra, Talen, PSEG) whose MWh are now contracted to hyperscalers.

## FINAL RANKING (written 2026-10-03)
1. Uprate / lost-MW recovery software (DVR + ML diagnostics + heat-balance surrogate), sold to merchant nuclear owners whose MWh are now contracted to hyperscalers.
2. Nuclear-grade manufacturing quality copilot for shops entering the supply chain (inspection-data + CMTR + traveler/NCR + ASME data reports), riding AP1000 $80B / $17.5B loan and SMR builds.
3. Radioligand dose orchestration for emerging RLT sponsors / radiopharmacies (batch-to-patient allocation under decay, reschedules, waste) - big money, real pain, but medtech incumbents moving in.
Honorable: performance-based nuclear security autonomy (rule pending); waste characterisation-to-WAC packages (gov sales cycle).
Skip: co-location interface (few buyers, consultant work, deals moved front-of-meter), rad-hard electronics (hardware qual, tiny TAM).
