Top three in detail

1 · Orphan patents with evidence of use

Frame
Own an unread patent from a bankrupt estate and sell it with a claim chart. Discovery is about 85% of this rung; a teardown costs $5-20k.
Step-up
3-30x. A lapse-bound patent costs only its $3.3-8.3k maintenance fee. The median sold asset was $33k in 2023 and $95k in 2024, and evidence of use adds 32-108% (Richardson Oliver 2025).
Hit rate
About 1-5% read on a live product (estimate). A hit costs $100k-1M today, and ML could cut that 5-20x. Patlytics ($40M Series B) already does this.
Data
USPTO bulk data, FCC internal photos, PACER section 363 schedules. Whether a patent reads on a product is not written in any field.
Right
Bought by assignment under 35 USC 261 or a section 363 sale. Brokers handle 87% of sales.
Ladder
Chart and sell, then license, assert, and run a fund. The ceiling is an Acacia or IV, a few billion dollars.

2 · Powered-land options

Frame
Option farmland near a substation and prove headroom. Discovery is only 30-40% of this rung; collateral, zoning, water and fibre are the rest (Texas SB6).
Step-up
A study alone earns 2-5x on $5,830/acre cropland (USDA 2025). The jump comes with rezoning and the service agreement.
Hit rate
A study costs at least $100k and succeeds 15-30% of the time (estimate), so the lever is 3-6x.
Data
LandGate already maps headroom at 84k+ substations.
Ladder
Real but capital-heavy: study, service agreement, shell, substation, campus. Cloverleaf and Tract are already there.

3 · Continuation Forge

Frame
Buy families with live continuations and draft claims onto shipping products. Prosecution costs about $800 in fees plus $5-40k for the attorney.
Step-up
5-30x on purchase price. The median sold family asked $158k.
Hit rate
Allowance runs 70-90%. A claim that is allowed and sells for 10x or more is about 5-15%. ML helps only with matching.
Data
Continuation status is a legible field, and brokers list it as a quality factor.

The law

Cheap proof and rare success never meet in human-made systems. Filings, studies and charts usually succeed (p 0.5-0.9), so the lever is 1.1-3x. Steps that usually fail (trials, restorations, lawsuits) are the expensive bespoke build, so no 80%-discovery frame exists.
Someone writes the outcome down. PHMSA status, AMSC codes, RIBITS, the 5-year land supply, NRC decommissioning filings, SoundExchange lists. Nature records nothing about where the ore is.
A 10^3x step-up survives only where bidders cannot see the unproven state. In open human markets, hits get bid away first: a $500-800 Rembrandt hammered at $870k. So the 10^4 structure appears only on tiny pools such as art and fossils.
Geology passes all three at once. The drill is a cheap binary proof that usually fails. Nature hides the answer and nobody summarises it. The state sells tenure cheaply. Developers pay at once. That is why Terranox works, and why nine hunts keep converging back on it.

There is no owner to sell the right. A sequence, structure or spectrum gets a first-come filing, often unpatentable as a composition (Myriad, product of nature). Physical finds such as fossils, meteorites and coins come from small pools with catalogued answer keys.
No buyer pays at the proof. Buyers pay after scale-up or field trials. Niron took 13 years and $150M+ for iron nitride. Merck's sitagliptin enzyme needed a 25,000-fold engineered gain over any wild type.
What geology alone has is an accepted proof standard. NI 43-101 and JORC make a drill intercept a regulated, comparable disclosure that markets reprice the day it is announced. Elsewhere the buyer redoes the proving, so the step-up never lands on the discoverer. The next hunt should look for where an accepted proof standard turns a cheap test into a price.

Recurring killers

  • Permits succeed anyway. Tower sites, water-right changes, zoning by right, hydro permits, land promotion.
  • The regulator wrote the answer down. Pipelines, defence parts, wetland credits, reactor restarts, airport slots.
  • The owner is the informed party. Tox packages, strain titres, engine records.
  • Open auctions bid hits away. Old Masters, Chinese ceramics.
  • The proof is only an opinion. Patents, art, attribution, face matching.
  • The physical build dominates. Pumped storage, reactor restarts ($1.5-2B), pipeline conversion, wetland restoration ($26k/acre), Phase 2 trials.
  • The right cannot be held before proving. Airport slots, ejido land, qui tam.
  • The pool is too small. Art sleepers ($100-300M/yr), meteorites, fossils.
