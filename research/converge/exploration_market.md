# R1: Market for ML-driven mineral (uranium) exploration, and how to make money
Research date: 2026-10-01. Every number carries source + date. Secondary sources (Discovery Alert, Crux, Sprott) flagged where primary not reached.

## 1. Uranium market state, 2026

**Price**
- Spot US$89.68/lb, long-term US$96.50/lb, Aug 2026 month-end (Cameco, UxC/TradeTech average) — https://www.cameco.com/invest/markets/uranium-price (accessed 2026-10-01).
- Spot ~US$90/lb on 15 Sep 2026; term ~US$96-96.50/lb = nominal all-time high (prior record US$95 in 2007); spot +19.6% YoY — Discovery Alert, 18 Sep 2026: https://discoveryalert.com/analysis/kazatomprom-uranium-production-outlook-september-2026/
- Jan 2026 spot peak US$101.41/lb; 6 Jul 2026 spot $85.50, term $94 — Bloodstone Capital, 8 Jul 2026: https://www.bloodstonecapital.co.uk/research/journal/uranium-supply-crunch-section-232-july-2026
- Sep 2025 reference: spot $82, term $82 — Sprott, 6 Oct 2025: https://sprott.com/insights/investors-act-with-conviction/
- Takeaway: term price is the highest it's ever been in nominal terms and sits ABOVE spot. That means utilities are paying up for long-dated security of supply. For an exploration company, the term price is the one that sets project NPVs.

**Supply gap / uncovered requirements** (secondary aggregation of UxC/WNA data)
- 2025 deficit 31 Mlb (173 Mlb mined vs 204 Mlb consumed); cumulative 2026-35 deficit 141 Mlb; 2040 "unidentified supply" 212 Mlb; Western utility uncovered requirements through 2035 ~1.4 billion lb — Discovery Alert, 21 Sep 2026: https://discoveryalert.com/analysis/uranium-supply-squeeze-structural-deficit-september-2026/
- WNA reference-case reactor requirements in 2040: 391 Mlb U3O8e, up from 175 Mlb in 2024 (+124%) — Sprott, 6 Oct 2025 (citing the WNA 2025 Nuclear Fuel Report): https://sprott.com/insights/investors-act-with-conviction/
- NexGen Rook I: funding gap of C$1.2B; average 21.7 Mlb/yr over a 10.7-year life; first production late 2029/2030. Even the largest undeveloped deposit covers only ~15% of the 2030 deficit — same Discovery Alert article, 21 Sep 2026.
- Takeaway: the gap after 2030 can't be closed with deposits already known. That is the case for discovery itself, not only for development.

**Kazatomprom**
- 2026 production guidance cut to 27,500-29,000 tU on a 100% basis (71.5-75.4 Mlb), against prior subsoil-use levels of 32,777 tU. That is ~3,000 tU / ~8 Mlb less, or ~5% of global supply. 2027 guidance is due Feb 2027 — Discovery Alert, 18 Sep 2026 (link above); WNN: https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/kazatomprom-to-lower-uranium-production-in-2026
- Kazakhstan imported 93.6% of its sulfuric acid from Russia (2023); ~3 Mlb of 2027 output is at risk from acid/sanction issues — Discovery Alert, 21 Sep 2026.
- Cameco 2026 guidance: Cigar Lake 17.5-18.0 Mlb; McArthur River 14.0-16.5 Mlb — Discovery Alert, 21 Sep 2026.

**US policy**
- Prohibiting Russian Uranium Imports Act: in force 11 Aug 2024 and runs to 31 Dec 2040. DOE waivers are capped (2026: 464,183 kg; 2027: 459,083 kg), and every waiver ends by 1 Jan 2028. Russia supplied ~25% of US enriched uranium — TradeTech: https://www.uranium.info/trade_restrictions.php (accessed 2026-10-01)
- Section 232 critical minerals: Proclamation 11001 of 15 Jan 2026 explicitly covers uranium. It imposed no tariffs. Instead it directed Commerce and USTR to negotiate agreements, which may include "price floors", with a status report due in 180 days (~mid-Jul 2026). Tariffs remain an option — Covington, Jan 2026: https://www.cov.com/en/news-and-insights/insights/2026/01/trump-administration-announces-results-of-critical-minerals-investigation-under-section-232-and-directs-us-officials-to-initiate-negotiations
- Uranium was added to the 2025 USGS Critical Minerals List. Proposals floated include government equity stakes in developers in exchange for offtake, and a domestic price floor. DOE awarded US$2.7B in enrichment contracts. US ISR restarts in April 2026: UEC Burke Hollow (TX) and Ur-Energy Shirley Basin (WY) — Bloodstone, 8 Jul 2026 (link above).
- Strategic stockpiling intent was stated by Energy Secretary Wright — Sprott, Oct 2025. I found no confirmed 2026 purchase volume.
- ADVANCE Act (signed Jul 2024): NRC is still implementing 30+ actions, mostly on reactor licensing, fees and foreign-ownership exceptions. Its effect on demand is indirect, with no direct effect on mining — Nuclear Innovation Alliance, May 2026: https://nuclearinnovationalliance.org/sites/default/files/2026-05/May%20Regulatory%20Implementation%20Summary%20NRC%20Progress%20Under%20the%20ADVANCE%20Act%20.pdf
- Takeaway for a founder: US policy now values domestic and allied pounds above the commodity price (price-floor talk, offtake-for-equity). US and Canadian discoveries are therefore worth more than equivalent ones in Kazakhstan, Niger or Namibia.

## 2. Uranium exploration spend and who the buyers are

**Global**
- Global uranium exploration plus mine development spend was ~US$380M in 2020 (trough), US$800M in 2022 and US$840M in 2023 (preliminary). Canada took 34% of 2021-23 spend. Canada, China, Russia, India, Namibia and Uzbekistan together took 90% — WNN on Red Book 2024, 8 Apr 2025: https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/invest-now-to-secure-long-term-uranium-future-red-book-says
- New Red Book (Sep 2026): exploration plus development topped US$1.78B over 2023-24, i.e. ~US$0.9B/yr, up 46% on 2021-22. Drilling for *new* projects is "relatively stagnant" — ANS, 15 Sep 2026: https://www.ans.org/news/2026-09-15/article-8391/newest-red-book-notes-the-need-for-more-u-investment/
- For context, all non-ferrous exploration was US$12.40B in 2025, down 0.6%; gold 50%, copper 26%, lithium 5%. Uranium isn't broken out — it sits in "other" — S&P World Exploration Trends 2026, via MiningVisuals, 24 Mar 2026: https://www.miningvisuals.com/post/2025-global-exploration-budget-by-commodity
- **Implication: the whole uranium exploration wallet is under ~US$1B/yr worldwide. Only part of that is greenfield/brownfield exploration, as opposed to development. Software can capture only a small share of it.**

**Canada / Athabasca**
- Canadian uranium exploration plus deposit appraisal: C$458M in 2024 (+21%) and C$458M in 2025 (preliminary). **2026 spending intentions are C$737M (+61%)**, about 14% of all Canadian exploration. Saskatchewan is the main destination — NRCan Canadian Mineral Exploration Information Bulletin, 7 Apr 2026: https://natural-resources.canada.ca/minerals-mining/mining-data-statistics-analysis/minerals-mining-publications/canadian-mineral-exploration-information-bulletin

**United States**
- US uranium expenditures (EIA-851A): 2023 total $107.4M (drilling $28.5M, production $22.5M, land/other $56.4M); 2024 $160.0M (drilling $53.7M); 2025 $234.7M (drilling $56.1M, production $92.3M, land/other $86.3M) — EIA 2025 Domestic Uranium Production Report, Jun 2026: https://www.eia.gov/uranium/production/annual/pdf/uexpenditurestbl8.pdf
- US production more than tripled in 2025 to the highest level since 2017 — EIA: https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=68044

**Australia**
- Uranium exploration spend was A$71M in 2024, the highest in over a decade. Production was 4,656 tU in 2024 (4th globally, 8%). Mines: Olympic Dam, Four Mile and Honeymoon (Boss) — Geoscience Australia AECR 2026: https://www.ga.gov.au/aecr2026/uranium-and-thorium

**Kazakhstan / Namibia**
- Kazakhstan exploration is state-controlled. Kazatomprom and its JV partners hold the subsoil rights, so it is not an open market for a startup. Namibia is in the Red Book top-6 by spend (WNN, Apr 2025). Its spend is dominated by Chinese-owned Husab/Rössing and by developers Paladin (Langer Heinrich), Deep Yellow (Tumas) and Bannerman (Etango). I could not find a 2025 Namibia-only dollar figure.

**Buyer universe (structure, not a count)**
- *Majors* (Cameco, Orano, Kazatomprom, CGN/CNNC): they run in-house geoscience teams and spend tens of millions a year each on exploration. They are slow, but they buy services and pilot software, and they are acquirers.
- *Developers* (NexGen, Denison, Paladin, which acquired Fission in 2024, IsoEnergy, F3, enCore, UEC, Ur-Energy, Boss, Deep Yellow): their capital goes overwhelmingly to permitting and construction. Examples: Denison's Phoenix ISR capex is ~C$600M (Simply Wall St, 2026) and NexGen faces a C$1.2B funding gap (Discovery Alert, 21 Sep 2026). Exploration is secondary for them. Denison is increasingly **farming out early-stage ground to juniors (e.g., Cosa Resources) for equity and JV stakes**. That is a prospect-generator pattern — https://simplywall.st/stocks/ca/energy/tsx-dml/denison-mines-shares/news/is-denison-mines-tsxdml-quietly-recasting-its-athabasca-role
- *Juniors*: there are dozens on the TSXV, CSE and ASX, many with Athabasca or Thelon ground (e.g., Skyharbour, Cosa, ATHA, Purepoint, Standard Uranium). They are financed by flow-through shares and typically spend C$2-15M per season, almost all of it on drilling. They are cash-constrained and slow to pay, and their marketing value comes from "AI-generated targets". In practice they pay for drill holes, not for software.
- **Implication: few customers, a small wallet, and the money concentrated in drilling.** A pure SaaS seller to uranium explorers faces a total market in the low tens of millions of dollars a year. Value capture has to be tied to discoveries (equity or royalties) or to owning ground.

**Pattern**
- The **large outcomes went to companies that own the ground**: KoBold at ~US$3B and Earth AI. VerAI is a hybrid prospect generator.
- **Pure software/services businesses exit small**: GoldSpot to ALS for C$30M, Datarock to IMDEX at ~A$60M implied. Or they pivot (EarthLabs became a media company). Or they stay small-revenue (VRIFY at 26 DORA clients).
- Software that wins usually comes bundled with hardware or data acquisition (Fleet Space at ~US$525M). Pure prospectivity mapping does not show the same result.
- **YC has already funded an AI uranium discovery company in W26 (Terranox), with the same vertically integrated thesis.** YC partners will ask "how are you different from Terranox?" The founder needs a sharp answer, for example: existing revenue or models already deployed; a specific district, data or land position; a different stage of the value chain (ISR/US roll-front vs Athabasca unconformity); or real drill results.

## 4. Has ML actually produced discoveries? How is success measured?

**KoBold, Mingomba (Zambia, copper)**: the strongest case, but it is **brownfield**.
- The license dated back to drilling in the 1950s and had never been developed. KoBold acquired it in late 2022.
- KoBold drilled ~34,000 m in the first year with up to 6 rigs, to depths of 1,800-2,000 m, and announced the discovery in Feb 2024. It was described as comparable to Kamoa-Kakula in size and grade — Fortune, 5 Feb 2024: https://fortune.com/2024/02/05/bill-gates-jeff-bezos-jack-ma-breakthrough-energy-venture-kobold-metal-copper-deposit-discovery ; Mongolian Mining Journal, 29 May 2024: https://www.mongolianminingjournal.com/a/73483
- What the AI actually did: unsupervised anomaly detection, supervised 3D geological modelling, and an "Intelligent Prospector" agent that sequenced drill holes over ~8 months instead of grid drilling. Senior economic geologists generated the structural hypotheses (MMJ, May 2024). **The ML mostly made delineation of a known system efficient. It was not a blind greenfield prediction.**
- Next steps: production target ~300 ktpa Cu, early 2030s; capex US$2.3-2.5B; KoBold has never built a mine; ZCCM wants 25% — Forbes, 5 May 2026.

**Earth AI (Australia)**
- Claims new systems in Cu, Co, Au, Ag, Mo and Sn in the NT and NSW (Mar 2025): https://theaiinsider.tech/2025/03/27/earth-ai-discovers-new-mineral-deposits-in-australia-using-ai-powered-exploration/
- Indium at Kooranjie, NSW: rock chips up to 117 ppm In, many above 20 ppm, exposed over 250 m and traceable ~3 km (15 May 2025): https://www.globalminingreview.com/mining/15052025/rare-high-grade-indium-find-validates-earth-ais-exploration-technology/
- A new gold system at Willow Glen (Jan 2025): https://www.globalminingreview.com/mining/02012025/earth-ai-discovers-new-gold-system-at-willow-glen/
- A palladium system with Legacy Minerals (Nov 2024).
- Claimed hit rate: "3 of 4 locations yield economic-grade minerals" vs "1 in 200" for the industry (Latitude Media). These are **mineralised occurrences and rock-chip/drill intercepts, not JORC resources**. No Earth AI discovery has yet been converted into a defined resource or sold to a major (as far as I can find).

**VRIFY/DORA**: Southern Cross Gold credits DORA in its Sunday Creek gold work (Sep 2024) — CMJ, Feb 2025. Sunday Creek was already a discovery, so this was extension targeting.

**Fleet ExoSphere**: IsoEnergy got 6 new high-priority targets on strike from Hurricane. Earlier drilling confirmed hydrothermal alteration there, but no new uranium deposit is announced (Sep 2024).

**Uranium-specific ML**: F3 Uranium says it uses AI/ML among its drill-targeting tools. The JR Zone hit 4.5 m at 50.1% U3O8 (Feb 2025), but it is not attributable to ML specifically — Oregon Group, 19 Mar 2025. Future Fuels/VRIFY (Hornby Basin) has published targets only, as of Jun 2026. Terranox (YC W26) has disclosed no drill results. **I found no publicly credited ML-led uranium deposit discovery.** That gap is the opportunity, and it is also the burden of proof.

**Base rates and timelines**
- Discovery-to-production averages ~29 years in the US, ~20 in Australia, 34 in Zambia, 10-15 in Ghana, DRC and Laos — S&P via Mining.com (2024): https://www.mining.com/us-has-second-longest-mine-development-timeline-in-the-world-sp-global-says/
- Copper: 263 major discoveries in 1990-2025, with the pipeline "thinning" — S&P, 3 Sep 2026: https://www.spglobal.com/market-intelligence/en/news-insights/research/2026/09/copper-discoveries-2026-expansion-over-addition-as-new-discoveries-lag
- Gold discovery cost per ounce has more than doubled from under US$20/oz two decades earlier (MinEx/CIM, Feb 2019): https://magazine.cim.org/en/technology/innovation-to-improve-the-exploration-odds-en/
- Uranium exploration "hit rate below 1%" is Terranox's framing (YC launch, 2026). I could not trace this to an authoritative primary dataset.

**How success should be measured** (analysis — use this to make the founder's claims credible)
1. *Target-level hit rate*: the share of drilled ML targets that return an intercept above a pre-declared threshold. For Athabasca: hole-probe cps and an assayed %U3O8 x m. For ISR roll-fronts: grade-thickness (GT) of at least 0.2-0.25. Compare against the same company's or district's non-ML holes drilled in the same season.
2. *Retrospective/blind backtests*: hide known deposits or later-dated holes, then measure the share of deposits captured in the top X% of ranked area (a capture-efficiency or AUC-type curve). This is cheap, credible to YC and to geologists, and can be done before any drilling.
3. *Cost per drill-metre to first significant intercept*, and *discovery cost per lb in resource*. These are the economic metrics juniors and majors use.
4. *Resource conversion*: an NI 43-101 or JORC inferred resource. This is the only measure the capital markets fully believe. It takes 2-5 years.
- Pre-register predictions publicly (timestamped target lists) before drilling. This removes the "cherry-picked hindsight" objection that hangs over most AI exploration claims.

## 5. Which business model the evidence favours

**Prospect generators: how the economics work**
- **The template is Renaissance Gold → Orogen → the Silicon/Arthur royalty.**
  - A prospect generator (Renaissance Gold) staked Silicon in Nevada and optioned it to AngloGold for **US$3M over 36 months**, retaining a **1% NSR** over ~15,000 acres. The option was completed in 2020: https://orogenroyalties.p15.adnetcms.com/news-releases/renaissance-gold-receives-formal-notification-from-anglogold-ashanti-to-complete-option-to-purchase-the-silicon-project/
  - AngloGold then discovered Merlin: **12.1 Moz inferred**, plus 3.4 Moz indicated at Silicon.
  - In Apr 2025 Triple Flag bought Orogen, whose core asset was that 1% NSR, for **C$421M** — Mining Weekly, 23 Apr 2025: https://www.miningweekly.com/article/triple-flag-to-acquire-orogen-royalties-for-c421m-in-expanded-silicon-gold-deal-2025-04-23
  - Altius sold **1% of its Arthur royalty to Franco-Nevada for US$250M** (+US$25M contingent), booking a C$374.5M gain in 2025 — Streetwise, 4 Aug 2025: https://www.streetwisereports.com/article/2025/08/04/franco-buys-altius-royalty-in-a-win-win-transaction.html ; Altius FY2025, 10 Mar 2026: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260310188675/en/Altius-Reports-2025-Attributable-Royalty-Revenue-of-$69.9M-and-Adjusted-Earnings1-of-$22.5M
  - **One generated property became a royalty worth ~US$250-300M per 1% NSR.** The power law is extreme.
- **Altius** (project generation plus royalties): 2025 attributable royalty revenue C$69.9M, adjusted EBITDA C$45.8M, year-end cash C$294M. It acquired Lithium Royalty Corp in Mar 2026. It runs a junior-equity portfolio built by vending generated projects for shares (Altius FY2025, link above).
- **EMX Royalty**: 2025 guidance was adjusted royalty revenue of US$26-32M and 10-12k GEOs. Q1 2025 adjusted royalty revenue was US$10.8M (+40% YoY) — SEC 6-K, May 2025: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1285786/000106299325009112/exhibit99-1.htm
  - It **merged with Elemental Altus on 13 Nov 2025** to form Elemental Royalty Corp: 200+ royalties, 16 producing. Tether invested ~US$100M — https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/elemental-altus-and-emx-announce-completion-merger-2025-11-13
  - Lesson: it took ~20 years of generating plus buying royalties to reach ~US$30M/yr. Generation alone is slow cash. The upside comes from occasional big hits and from recycling option cash into producing royalties.
- **Uranium prospect generators in the Athabasca, operating today**
  - **Skyharbour**: 680k+ ha. Partners have committed **>C$79M in partner-funded exploration plus >C$52M in cash and share payments** to Skyharbour. Partners include Orano, Denison and ~7 earn-in juniors (Nexus, North Shore, UraEx, Future Fuels, Mustang, Purecore, Terra Clean) — https://skyharbourltd.com/projects/prospect-generator-model/ (accessed 2026-10-01)
  - **CanAlaska**: ~500k ha project-generator portfolio. It sole-funded into the Cameco JV at West McArthur, reaching 88.86%, and made the **Pike Zone discovery (8.6 m @ 34.59% U3O8; 14.8 m @ 14.71%)**. Its 2026 program is C$15M — 6 Jan 2026: https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/markets/stocks/CVV-X/pressreleases/36902545/canalaska-plans-large-2026-exploration-program-at-west-mcarthur-joint-venture/
  - **Denison** is vending early-stage ground to Cosa for equity and JV interests (2026).
  - Saskatchewan's online MARS staking cut claim approval from ≥30 to 5 business days. It allowed 458,000 ha to be staked in 27 hours during a 2013 uranium rush. Land is fast to acquire, so good AI-generated ground can be locked up quickly — https://www.miningandenergy.ca/read/mineral-dispositions-in-saskatchewan-skyrockets
