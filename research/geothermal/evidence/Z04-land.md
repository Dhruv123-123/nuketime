# Z04 — Zanskar land position: BLM geothermal leases, lease-sale wins, acres, states

**Scope:** Which federal (BLM) geothermal leases Zanskar Geothermal & Minerals Inc. won at auction from 2021 to 2026, what it paid, where the leases are, and what this tells a "Terranox for geothermal" company.

**Method:** The general web-search budget ran out at the start of this task, so this file rests almost entirely on **primary BLM documents**, downloaded directly and parsed. Those documents are the Nevada, Utah and New Mexico sale notices, registered-bidder lists, all-bids reports and sale summaries. Zanskar's own press releases fill in the rest. MLRS/LR2000 was not queried interactively, so non-auction leases (noncompetitive filings, assignments, private or state leases) are **not** captured. Per-parcel bonus totals are **computed** as high bid per acre × acres, rounded up the way BLM bills it. They are not BLM-published per-parcel totals, so treat them as ±1%.

## 2. Sale by sale

### Nevada, 30 Aug 2022 (22 registered bidders; 192,912 acres received bids; $2.98M in high bids; top bid $111/acre)
Zanskar won 3 parcels covering 8,609 acres for about $139.8k:
- **NV-2022-08-1859**: 2,848.92 ac, Humboldt Co., T33–34N R40E, inside the **"Pumpernickel and Rock Creek Allotments"**. Won at **$41/acre** (~$116.8k). This is almost certainly the **Pumpernickel** discovery that Zanskar announced in Sep 2025 (inferred from the grazing allotment name and the Humboldt/Winnemucca location).
- **NV-2022-08-1866**: 2,240 ac, and **NV-2022-08-1867**: 3,520 ac. Both are in Pershing Co., T33N R25E (C-Punch Ranch, near Lovelock), won at **$4/acre** against KDS Investments' $3.
- Zanskar bid on 9 parcels and won 3. Ormat (12 wins), Aspect Holdings (13) and Baseload Power (10) took the most parcels.

### Nevada, 14 Nov 2023 (13 registered bidders; 96,606 acres received bids; $826k in high bids; top bid $130/acre)
Zanskar won **6 parcels covering 16,720 acres, every one at the statutory minimum of $2/acre (~$33.4k total)**:
- **NV-2023-11-6986**: 3,778.86 ac, Pershing Co., T33N R25–26E. It adjoins the 2022 Lovelock-area block. Zanskar says it identified the **Big Blind** anomaly in "late 2023" and got the land as "BLM leases at auction". Big Blind is in the "western Nevada desert", so this block is a plausible Big Blind candidate (inference, unverified).
- **NV-2023-11-1961, -6988, -1965, -1971, -1959**: 2,941.54 + 2,468.08 + 2,440 + 2,539.56 + 2,551.91 ac, Humboldt Co., T34–35N R40–42E. This is a contiguous step-out from the 2022 Pumpernickel parcel, and each parcel drew only Zanskar's bid.
- Zanskar won 6 of the 7 parcels it bid on, and was the second-largest winner by parcel count after TLS Geothermics (8).

### Nevada, 8 Oct 2024 (36 registered bidders; 217,866 acres received bids; $7.42M in high bids; top bid $202/acre; $7.86M receipts)
Zanskar won **7 parcels covering 23,821 acres for about $575k**:
- **NV-2024-10-2048**: 4,961.97 ac, Lander Co., T17N R39E, won at **$61/acre** (~$302.7k) over Invenergy's $60. On the adjoining **NV-2024-10-2049** (3,742.75 ac, same township), Invenergy ("IVENERGY ENERGY TRANSITION LLC") beat Zanskar's **$111** with a **$112/acre** bid.
- **NV-2024-10-7026** (3,840 ac, $22/acre) and **-2059** (1,920 ac, $15/acre): Lander Co., T24N R47E.
- **NV-2024-10-7019** (4,554.10 ac, $13/acre) and **-2065** (3,762.64 ac, $14/acre): Mineral Co., T5N R30–31E.
- **NV-2024-10-7022**: 3,120 ac, Lyon/Douglas Co., T12N R23E, won at $13/acre over Invenergy's $12 and Ormat's $11.
- **NV-2024-10-7028**: 1,662.6 ac, Pershing Co., T33N R33E, $4/acre.
- Zanskar bid on 11 parcels and won 7. Buffalo River Minerals (28 wins) and Invenergy (17) dominated this sale.

### Nevada, 21 Oct 2025 (22 registered bidders; 280,559 acres received bids; $8.87M in high bids; **top bid $410/acre**; top parcel $2.10M)
Zanskar won **only 2 parcels covering 872 acres for about $16k**:
- **NV-2025-10-7039**: 139.16 ac, Pershing Co., T33N R33E (a fill-in beside the 2024 parcel 7028), $4/acre.
- **NV-2025-10-2174**: 732.63 ac of split estate, Eureka/Lander Co., T23–24N R48E (a step-out beside the 2024 T24N R47E block), won at **$21/acre**. Invenergy and Skyladder Capital were the other bidders.
- **It lost NV-2025-10-2143**: 1,758.54 ac, Humboldt Co., **T34N R41E**, right inside the Pumpernickel block. **LITHOS48 INC took it at $232/acre (~$408k) over Zanskar's $231.** Invenergy went to $175 and Navram Resources to $45.
- **This is the key data point.** Zanskar bought the Pumpernickel step-out acreage at **$2/acre in Nov 2023**. A month after it publicly announced the Pumpernickel discovery (Sep 2025), adjacent acreage cleared at **$232/acre, a ~116× step-up**, and Zanskar lost it. The bid run shows that public disclosure of a discovery reprices nearby land almost at once.

## 3. Zanskar's share and bidding style

| Sale | Zanskar acres | Share of acres that received bids | Zanskar bonus | Share of high-bid dollars | Avg $/acre (Zanskar) | Avg $/acre (sale) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NV 2022 | 8,609 | 4.5% | ~$140k | 4.7% | ~$16 | ~$15 |
| NV 2023 | 16,720 | 17.3% | ~$33k | 4.0% | $2 | ~$8.5 |
| NV 2024 | 23,821 | 10.9% | ~$575k | 7.8% | ~$24 | ~$34 |
| NV 2025 | 872 | 0.3% | ~$16k | 0.2% | ~$18 | ~$32 |
| **Total** | **~50,022** | | **~$764k** | | **~$15** | |

The pattern is consistent:
1. **It buys quietly at or near the minimum.** 12 of its 18 wins cleared at ≤$14/acre and 6 at the $2 floor.
2. **It clusters and steps out.** The leases form tight blocks: Pumpernickel (Humboldt, 2022→2023), Lovelock (Pershing T33N R25E, 2022→2023), Pershing T33N R33E (2024→2025) and Lander/Eureka T24N (2024→2025). That is classic "win the core, then fill in around it".
3. **It is price-disciplined.** It loses close bidding wars by $1/acre: $111 vs $112 (2024) and $231 vs $232 (2025). It also skipped Utah at $100–344/acre.
4. **Its pace collapsed in 2025.** Only 872 acres were added, because competitors (Invenergy, Ormat, Cordillera, Skyladder, Navram, Lithos48, Buffalo River) flooded the auctions and the top bid hit $410/acre.

**The market got crowded fast.** NV sale high-bid dollars went $1.45M (2021) → $2.98M (2022) → $0.83M (2023) → $7.42M (2024) → $8.87M (2025) → $4.44M (2026). The NM Jun-2026 sale took in $16.6M. Several winners (Buffalo River Minerals, Percheron Professional Services, Umbrella Renewables, Cordillera, Skyladder Capital, Lithos48) are LLCs with no public operating identity. They are probably nominee or land-banking vehicles for developers or speculators (unverified). **Land-banking ahead of discovery is already happening in the BLM channel.**

## 5. Context: what Zanskar has built on this land
- **Equity raised:** $180M in total, including a $115M Series C led by Spring Lane in Jan 2026. Zanskar also has a $40M non-recourse development facility that can grow to $100M (Just Climate and Spring Lane).
- **Offtake:** a 100 MW PPA with NV Energy, 20 years through 2050, delivery by 2030, subject to approval of the 2026 IRP. It covers a portfolio of Nevada projects (Pumpernickel, Big Blind and "others").
- **Big Blind well:** total depth of 2,668.5 ft, reservoir at ~250°F and ~2,700 ft, with no surface manifestation. Zanskar claims a discovery cost **< $15k/MW**, against **> $100k/MW** for blind finds in the 1970s and 80s.
- **Lightning Dock:** ~15 MW gross plant, acquired Jun 2024. A new well (44-7) at ~8,000 ft flows > 4,000 gpm with a productivity index of ~50 gpm/psi.
- **DOE:** two project selections in Sep 2026, one at Lightning Dock (horizontal injection well) and one at an undrilled Nevada site. Award amounts are not disclosed.

## Implications for a Terranox-shape geothermal company and the gap vs Zanskar

1. **Buy cheap before proof, then capture the step-up: the arbitrage is real and now measured.** Zanskar bought Pumpernickel step-out acreage at **$2/acre** (2023). After disclosure, adjacent acreage cleared at **$232/acre** (2025), and Utah land near Fervo cleared at up to **$344/acre**. Land repriced roughly 100× before any MW was proven. The entire Zanskar auction position cost **under $1M in bonus bids for ~50k acres**. For Terranox-style capital the entry ticket is tiny. The value comes from picking the right parcels, which is exactly the ML edge.

2. **The gap: Zanskar is a developer that uses land as an input. It is not a land owner that monetizes the step-up.** Its land is narrow (about 18 auction parcels, a single state for auctions), it has pivoted to building plants (100 MW PPA, plant acquisition, project-finance facility), and its 2025 acreage additions nearly stopped. A pure "prospect-generator / royalty" company could do the following:
   - hold a **far broader** set of option-like positions (hundreds of parcels across NV, UT, NM, OR, ID and CA);
   - **farm out or sell** proven or de-risked blocks to Fervo, Ormat, Invenergy and utilities while **retaining an overriding royalty**, the way prospect generators work in mining;
   - avoid the capex and execution risk Zanskar now carries.
   Zanskar cannot easily be the neutral seller of land to its own competitors (Ormat, Invenergy).

3. **Channels where Zanskar is absent or thin:**
   - (a) **Utah, New Mexico, Oregon, Idaho and California BLM/USFS sales.** It won nothing outside Nevada.
   - (b) **Noncompetitive day-after filings.** In NV-2025 only **1 of 27** leftover parcels (4,890 of 97,120 acres) drew a noncompetitive offer. That is ~92k acres of already-nominated, already-NEPA'd land left on the table at no bonus.
   - (c) **Private fee geothermal rights and split-estate** (e.g. ranch land; parcel 2174 was split estate).
   - (d) **State trust land:** Utah SITLA, New Mexico State Land Office, Arizona, Idaho.
   - (e) **Lease nominations.** BLM sales are driven by nominations, so every nomination advertises interest. A company that nominates broadly and bids at the $2 floor across many basins hides its signal in noise.

4. **Main risk revealed: the information leak and competition for land.** Discovery announcements reprice adjacent land within weeks. Specialist land bankers (Buffalo River, Percheron, Cordillera, Lithos48) are already in the auctions. A Terranox-shape company must:
   - lock up the **whole fairway** before any well or press release;
   - use nominations and noncompetitive filings to avoid tipping its hand;
   - treat **speed of acquisition** as a core capability.
   Zanskar's $1 losses on the Pumpernickel and Lander step-outs show that even the leader is getting crowded out of its own trends.

5. **Scale check (it is not "too small"):** BLM manages geothermal leasing on about **245M acres**, and BLM-land plants already total **2.65 GW** across 51 plants. Nevada alone offered 66–113 parcels and 219k–378k acres per year in 2024–2026. Recent sales cleared at $100–400/acre on hot ground, against $2 on blind ground. An ML-driven buyer of blind acreage at floor prices therefore has a large, recurring, cheap inventory to buy from, plus a visible resale market.
