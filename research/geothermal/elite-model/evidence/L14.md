## What the field knows

**BEL is a protocol, not an algorithm.** Yin, Strebelle and Caers (2020, GMD 13:651, AutoBEL) give the six steps: (1) state the decision and the prediction variable; (2) build a broad Monte Carlo prior; (3) falsify the prior against the data; (4) run global sensitivity analysis (DGSA); (5) learn a direct map from data to prediction across prior realizations; (6) falsify the posterior before deciding. Step 5 skips inversion. Data and prediction are each reduced (PCA; signed distance for categorical rock types), made Gaussian (normal score), paired with canonical correlation analysis (CCA), and conditioned with a closed-form Gaussian regression. Updating runs in order: structure, then rock type, then properties, then fluids. https://gmd.copernicus.org/articles/13/651/2020/gmd-13-651-2020.html

**The school's textbook.** Scheidt, Li and Caers (2018), *Quantifying Uncertainty in Subsurface Systems* (AGU-Wiley). A follow-up on decision making under uncertainty is cited as Caers, Scheidt and Li (2026) (unverified title).

**Geothermal BEL exists, but only at prospect scale.**
- Athens and Caers (2019, Applied Energy 256, 113932) turned geologic understanding of a basin-scale extensional system into a prior, ran Monte Carlo heat-flow models, and used BEL with CCA to predict temperature from one temperature well, without inversion. Sensitivity showed basement and basin permeability and basal heat flux control target temperature. The companion SGW 2018 paper ran 800 COMSOL models and kept 592 (see L11). https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/appene/v256y2019ics0306261919316198.html
- Hermans, Nguyen, Klepikova, Dassargues and Caers (2018, WRR 54:2931) predicted heat-storage behaviour of an aquifer from cross-borehole ERT using 500 prior realizations and about 1,000 forward runs (5 minutes each). MCMC would need tens to hundreds of thousands. https://ngi.stanford.edu/sites/ngi/files/media/file/2017wr022135.pdf
- Robin Thibaut (Ghent BEL group) co-authored Zanskar's 2026 surrogate paper (M13). Zanskar's "Bayesian evidential learning" claim most likely means this lineage.

**Mineral-X moved BEL into decisions.** Stanford Mineral-X (Caers, Kochenderfer, Mukerji, with KoBold Metals) frames exploration as a partially observable Markov decision process (POMDP). Key papers (list from https://mineralx.stanford.edu/scientific-research):
- Caers, Scheidt, Yin, Wang, Mukerji and House (2022, NRR), "Efficacy of information in mineral exploration drilling". Efficacy of information is the expected reduction of uncertainty on a quantity of interest from a planned measurement, before the measurement is made. It is VOI without a payoff function.
- Hall, Scheidt, Wang, Yin, Mukerji and Caers (2022, NRR 31(5):2413), "Sequential value of information for subsurface exploration drilling".
- Mern and Caers (2023, GMD 16:289), "Intelligent Prospector v1.0": hierarchical Bayesian particle filter with a Gaussian-process background, planned by POMCPOW tree search. https://gmd.copernicus.org/articles/16/289/2023/gmd-16-289-2023.pdf
- Mern, Corso, Burch, House and Caers (2024, arXiv 2410.10610), "Intelligent Prospector v2.0", adds uncertainty over which geological model is true.
- Moss, Corso, Caers and Kochenderfer (2024), "BetaZero": learned belief-state planning for long-horizon POMDPs.
- Athens and Caers (2022, Math Geosci 54:413): stochastic gravity inversion with structural uncertainty. Wei et al. (2024, Surv Geophys) build geophysical priors from geochemistry. Yin et al. (2025, IEEE TGRS) quantify "model misrepresentation" in inversion.
- Caers (2025, arXiv 2512.02879), "The future of AI in critical mineral exploration": deterministic interpolation has about half the variance of the real borehole data, which inflates predictor-label correlation and drives false-positive drilling. Fix: stochastic simulation plus Popper-style falsification. Priority order: falsify hypotheses first, then volume, then grade.

**Bayesian geostatistics for geothermal temperature.**
- Trainor-Guitton, Siler and Ayling (SGW 2023) built 50 sequential-simulation temperature realizations of Granite Springs Valley, NV, using 2 m probes, 6 gradient wells and 47 1970s shallow holes, with structural proxies as soft data (cokriging and local varying mean). They ranked 9 candidate wells by expected utility and by entropy. https://pangea.stanford.edu/ERE/pdf/IGAstandard/SGW/2023/Trainorguitton.pdf
- O'Malley et al. (LANL, arXiv 2605.16665, 2026), "In-Context Earth": a 830k-parameter transformer that takes about 20 nearby borehole readings as context and outputs a 128-bin temperature distribution. It beats universal kriging and is well calibrated. https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2605.16665.md

## Best methods and their reported skill

| Method | Test | Reported result |
|---|---|---|
| Intelligent Prospector v1 (POMCPOW + particle filter + GP) | Synthetic 2D ore body, 100 realizations, 25 max measurements | Decision accuracy 93-96%; 88-96% of profitable ore mined; 5.1-6.5 measurements per decision; matched a 16-point grid after about 7-10 measurements. Multi-body case wrongly abandoned about 32% of profitable deposits (particle-filter collapse) |
| BEL with CCA (Hermans 2018) | Field heat-tracer test, Belgium | CCA correlations 0.75/0.40 (1-day) and 0.89/0.72 (5-day); posterior spread near the 1.5 °C ERT limit; about 1,000 runs vs 10^4-10^5 for MCMC |
| AutoBEL (Yin 2020) | Synthetic gas field, 1.5M cells, 250 priors, 7 conditioning + 2 blind wells | Posterior not falsified by the 2 blind wells; large reduction in volume uncertainty (no single number in abstract) |
| Proxy cokriging (Trainor-Guitton 2023) | Granite Springs, NV | Proxy-temperature correlations: dilation -0.56, Coulomb 0.35, fault distance 0.29; LVM R² 0.56-0.68; variogram range about 2.7 km |
| In-Context Earth (LANL 2026) | US boreholes; Alberta, Australia, UK out of sample | KS 2.5% vs 5.9% baseline. MAE 4.7 °C vs kriging 5.1 °C in the US; out of sample 2.2/6.2/5.4 °C vs kriging 9.2/28.5/8.9 °C. MAE grows from 3.1 °C (0-2 km) to 11.2 °C (4-6 km) |
| Mineral-X field use | KoBold, Mingomba (Zambia) | A tier-1 copper discovery; Caers says drilling was "much more efficient" than a grid. No published numbers |

There is no published field hindcast of any of these on a geothermal *discovery* question. Every geothermal BEL paper is a single prospect with known geology.

## Datasets used

- Synthetic: Intelligent Prospector (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.6727378, code github.com/sisl/MineralExploration); AutoBEL synthetic Australian gas field (GitHub/Zenodo).
- Hermalle-sous-Argenteau heat-tracer and cross-hole ERT, Belgium.
- Granite Springs Valley, NV: 2 m probes, 6 TGHs, 47 legacy holes, 3D geologic model from gravity, MT and seismic (INGENIOUS-funded, DE-EE0009254).
- Dixie-style Basin and Range model (Athens and Caers 2018/2019).
- LANL: US borehole temperature compilation (>95% of wells have a single reading; about 3% of readings deeper than 4 km), plus Alberta, Australian and UK sets.
- Mineral-X: KoBold drillholes, EM surveys (19 receivers in the Caers 2025 example), Superior Craton pegmatite geochemistry (Scheidt 2024).

## What this means for our model design

- **Add a falsification gate before every fit.** Run BEL's prior-falsification test (robust Mahalanobis distance of real data against prior-simulated data, reject above the 97.5th percentile) for each basin. Run it again on the posterior with held-out wells. This catches a wrong mask class or wrong heat-flow prior before it poisons the Cox intensity.
- **Use BEL as the fast column and prospect update inside SENTINEL-COX.** For each mask class, simulate 1,000-10,000 1D or 2D fault-flow columns. Learn the map from "cheap readings" (2 m, ranch-well log, BHT) to "T at 1-3 km and system-present" with CCA or, better, a conditional normalizing flow. This replaces MCMC per cell, and it gives the Tier 1 observation physics a cheap posterior.
- **Use DGSA to prune the latent space.** Athens and Caers found only 3 of 14 parameters matter. Spend our latent dimensions on heat flux, basement permeability and conductivity, and fix the rest.
- **Make the decision engine a POMDP, not one-step VOI.** VOI-Bank ranks one action at a time. Intelligent Prospector shows tree search beats grids at about half the measurements. Our action set (probe, drone dart, MT line, lease bid, drop) fits a POMDP over a portfolio of cells. Start with BetaZero-style learned value functions to make the horizon tractable.
- **Carry uncertainty over the conceptual model itself.** IP v2 and Yin 2025 put a prior on which geological story is true. Our "shared latent mask class" should be a discrete model variable with its own posterior, so a lidded outflow plume and a deep upflow are scored as rival hypotheses.
- **Never feed kriged layers in as deterministic covariates.** Caers shows interpolation halves variance and creates false positives. Feed the GBM and Cox model with stochastic realizations, or propagate the layer's kriging variance.
- **Swap kriging for In-Context Earth as the temperature prior.** It is calibrated (KS 2.5%) and transfers across basins. Fine-tune it on Great Basin data with the effort tags.
- **Score sensors by efficacy of information before buying them.** Efficacy needs no payoff model, so it can rank probe designs before RNPV inputs are solid.

## Gaps nobody has filled that we could exploit with AI

1. **Regional BEL.** All geothermal BEL work is one prospect. Nobody runs BEL across thousands of cells with a shared prior. Amortized neural posterior estimation (one network for all cells, conditioned on the local covariates) makes this cheap.
2. **BEL with a discovery-process prior.** No BEL or POMDP paper models the search history (who drilled where, when). Our effort-thinned Cox count is a prior nobody else has.
3. **Prior falsification for regional ML maps.** Nobody has tested whether the USGS 2025 or Nevada PFA maps are falsified by later wells. An agent can run that test on each new GDR well as it posts.
4. **POMDP over land, not just holes.** Mineral-X plans drilling. Nobody plans lease bids, option payments and drops jointly with measurements. That is our business.
5. **Legacy logs as BEL data.** BEL assumes clean data. VLM-extracted scanned logs (M-files) add data noise that can be learned as part of the forward model.
6. **Published hindcast.** No group publishes a geothermal BEL or POMDP hindcast against real discoveries. Running one (Blue Mountain, Gabbs, Granite Springs, Big Blind) would be a first.
