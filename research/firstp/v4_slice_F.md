## Web checks on the top 3 (all legs except ML)

### 1. Flex micro-sites (17 → 16)
- **S1 holds.** Utility hosting-capacity maps are built for distributed generation. Con Edison's map is refreshed twice a year for PV, storage and DG, and New York is still only *recommending* features such as substation 8760 load data ([Con Edison FAQ](https://www.coned.com/en/business-partners/hosting-capacity/faq-page), [NY DPS](https://dps.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2025/05/hosting-capacity-map-feature-recommendations.pdf)). Load headroom needs a formal capacity inquiry ([Acres](https://www.acres.com/blog/transmission-line-proximity-vs.-substation-capacity-in-data-center-site-selection)). So inquiry outcomes really are private labels.
- **S5 weakens (4 → 3).** EPRI, Nvidia, Prologis and InfraPartners target 5-20 MW sites at substations with spare capacity, with at least 5 pilots in development by end-2026 ([HPCwire](https://www.hpcwire.com/off-the-wire/epri-launches-distributed-inference-data-center-pilot-with-nvidia-and-prologis/), [DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/nvidia-prologis-epri-infrapartners-target-prefab-data-centers-at-substation-sites/)). IEEE Spectrum reports ~25 sites across five utilities ([IEEE Spectrum](https://spectrum.ieee.org/distributed-inference-data-centers)). The end buyer is gathering the same labels with utilities directly (L8).
- **Still open (own-the-asset):** whether a tenantless SPV can hold and assign a 10 MW confirmation. That was untested in T36R and is not settled here.
- **Score: 3-3-3-4-3 = 16.** The kill test is T36R's: 30 inquiries plus the written assignment question.

### 2. DLA surplus legacy spares (17 → 15)
- **S1 holds.** Under its DLA contract, Liquidity Services paid ~4.35% of original acquisition value for all usable non-rolling surplus referred ([Liquidity Services](https://liquidityservices.com/press-releases/liquidity-services-inc-awarded-new-surplus-contract-us-0), 2014 terms, may be stale). GovPlanet (Ritchie Bros.) sold 75,000 DLA items in one year ([Equipment FA](https://www.equipmentfa.com/news/32025/ritchie-bros-extends-support-of-u-s-department-of-defense)). Prices are set per channel, not per item, which is the buy-before-known condition.
- **S2/S4 constrained.** USML items carry DEMIL B/C (mutilation to scrap), and end-use items need a TSC clearance (up to 60 days) plus a DLA Form 1822 every time ([DLA DEMIL codes](https://www.dla.mil/DispositionServices/DDSR/QuickLinks/demilcodes/), [DLA DEMIL coding](https://www.dla.mil/HQ/LogisticsOperations/Services/FIC/DEMILCoding)). The highest-value military spares are often exactly the ones you cannot resell intact.
- **Size unproven (rule).** The only volume figure found is GAO's FY1996: 3.3M usable aircraft parts sold, with $2.3B of acquisition value ([GAO NSIAD-98-7](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GAOREPORTS-NSIAD-98-7/html/GAOREPORTS-NSIAD-98-7.htm)). No current resale market size was found. The bulk channel is held by two contractors.
- **Score: 4-3-3-3-2 = 15.** The path to a large company is unproven.

### 3. Congestion-pocket sites + FTR hedge (16 → 15)
- **S2 decays.** Enverus reports that West Texas ultra-low prices are becoming rarer because price-sensitive data-centre load is arriving: 2.4 GW approved and 8-20 GW under study through 2027 ([Enverus](https://www.enverus.com/blog/the-impact-of-load-growth-and-datacenters-on-ercot-prices-a-new-era-for-west-texas)). The pocket a model finds is erased by the load that exploits it.
- **Regulatory tail.** Texas is reviewing whether flexible loads avoid too much transmission cost through 4CP ([TheEnergyMag](https://theenergymag.com/news/2026-06-11/texas-4cp-ai-bitcoin)). Miners report surprise congestion spikes and ancillary fees ([CoinGeek](https://coingeek.com/ercots-queue-creates-challenges-for-texas-btc-miners/)).
- **No 2026 FTR rule change found** in this search ([PJM Inside Lines](https://insidelines.pjm.com/?p=9897), older items only). QB's numbers stand: $4.27B of FTR profit, and the IMM's $5.4B shortfall to load is a political target.
- **Score: 3-2-4-3-3 = 15.** The best ML-plausibility idea in the slice, but it earns a decaying yield, not a step-up.
