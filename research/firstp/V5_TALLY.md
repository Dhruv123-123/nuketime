## Pick: AI-native surety carrier
- Market ~$12-13B/yr US premium; loss ratios ~20-24%; underwriting profit >$2B/yr for three years.
- AI cuts the expense ratio ~17-19 points (CNA Surety ran 54-55% in 2008-10) by reading contractor financials and job progress; one underwriter covers ~5x the accounts.
- Ladder: AI MGA on fronted paper (months) -> buy a Treasury-listed shell (Trisura/First Founders ~$18.8M, ~9 months) -> own carrier -> contractor lending from the same data.
- Exit comparable: Palomar paid $300M for Gray Surety (~$87M premium, ~3.4x premium) -> ~$5B at $1.5B premium.
- Risks: construction-cycle loss tail (Q1 2026 loss ratio up to 23.8%), InRev already live as an AI surety MGA, incumbents adopting the same AI.
- Test (6-10 weeks, $40-80k): back-test an AI underwriter on 300-500 historical bond files from a partner MGA/regional surety; kill if AUC lift <0.05 over their scores; plus fronting/reinsurance indications, price three listed shells, 20 agent calls.

## Pattern
AI counter-moves work where the killer is engineering, paperwork, underwriting or origination labour per unit. They fail where the killer is a gatekeeper's queue (DLA reviewers, utilities, PJM rules), physics of the asset (cell cost), or a priced market.
