# Terranox v2 principles (from the first-principles teardown, 19 Oct 2026)

The hunts' rubric copied one half of Terranox: public illegible data, a naive seller and a 10^4 step-up at proof. The teardown says the durable half is different. Score ideas on these five, 0-5 each (/25):

P1 Option before anyone knows: the right is bought cheaply before ANYONE (seller, buyer, you) knows its value. The seller need not be naive; nobody has the information yet. Price set by schedule, by indifference, or by a market with no information.
P2 Heavy tail plus fast repricing: a minority of rights become worth 100x+, and a standard proof makes the market reprice at once.
P3 Private outcome flywheel: every proof you run produces outcome data competitors cannot see (confidentiality window, or nobody else runs that proof). Your model improves faster than the public record. THIS replaces "public illegible data" as the moat; public data is only the cold start.
P4 Someone else pays for the expensive confirmation: a capitalised partner funds the costly step (earn-in, licence, offtake) on YOUR right while you keep a royalty or carry. The prospect-generator model (EMX, Altius in mining).
P5 Validatable before betting: enough historical labels to hindcast the model's lift against a simple baseline before spending on rights.

Standing rules still apply: no prospecting/drilling, not Terranox, own the asset, venture scale.
Note on earlier laws: L8 ("end buyer funds the proof, so no middle rung") becomes a FEATURE under P4 whenever you hold the right first. L3 ("seller prices hope") only bites when hope is legible; P1 needs rights priced before information exists.
