# V5: raise the ceiling (19 Oct 2026, 22:35Z)

Dhruv: "the scope you think of what's possible is too low. Increase your scope and optimism and find something that *could* work theoretically. The theoretical part should not be tech-wise, but rather numbers-wise, time-wise etc. If there are things up the vertical that kill it, find answers that leverage AI to solve those while still making the economics work."

## What changes
1. **Optimism on tech.** If it is physically possible and plausibly within reach of current or near-term AI and engineering, assume it works. Tech risk is not a kill.
2. **The bar is the numbers and the clock.** An idea passes only if a back-of-envelope model closes: market and price, unit economics, capital needed vs. what a seed/Series A/B company can raise (or project finance can carry), time to first revenue, time to $100M revenue and a $1B+ outcome. Numbers must be sourced or explicitly derived, not vibes.
3. **Vertical killers must be answered, not accepted.** Every earlier kill up the vertical (incumbent or PE holds the rung, end buyer goes direct, seller prices the hope, slow regulatory proof, capital-heavy build, licensing, labour cost, origination cost) gets an AI-leveraged counter-move: AI agents collapsing labour/origination cost so a roll-up or developer works at startup scale; AI compressing permitting, certification or registration timelines; AI underwriting risk others can't so capital is cheaper; AI letting a small team operate assets incumbents run with hundreds. The counter-move counts only if its effect on the numbers is stated (e.g., "cuts per-site development cost from $X to $Y, so IRR goes from A to B").
4. **Ambition first.** Prefer ideas whose ceiling is $10B+. A $100-200M/yr ceiling still fails ("too small").

## Standing rules that still hold
- No prospecting or drilling. Not Terranox.
- Own the valuable thing (asset, right, product, balance sheet), not a service shop. Public/illegible data + AI as an edge is preferred, not required.
- Sectors: largely energy, machines, robotics, deeptech, security tech; broad allowed.
