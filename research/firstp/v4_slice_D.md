# v4 slice D: rounds 4-7 re-scored on ML plausibility (19 Oct 2026)

Scope: every scored idea in `converge/gold/round4-7_results.json` plus the three round-7 theses. That is 234 scorings, merged to 206 distinct ideas (28 repeats across rounds share one row). Rubric is V4_PLAUSIBLE.md: S1 buy-before-known, S2 step-up 3x+, S3 ML plausibility (judgement, not web-checked), S4 discovery-heavy frame, S5 compounding edge. Each leg is scored 0-5, for a total out of 25. A pass needs 17+ with no leg at 0-1. The standing rules still apply: no prospecting or drilling, not Terranox, own the asset, and a path to a large company. Path is a gate column: Y, weak or N.

**Result: in this slice, nothing passes after web checks.** The only change v4 makes is the S3 leg. In rounds 4-7 the ML leg was not what killed these ideas. They died because the seller already prices the hope (S1), because a vendor already sells the signal (S5), or because the proving step is the heavy work (S4). Raising S3 lifts about 20 ideas by 1-2 points, and the best of them land at 16. Four ideas reach 17+, but each fails the path-to-a-large-company gate: they are collectibles or credit niches.

**3. USDA GRIN accession to trait line: 16, unchanged**
- S2: confirmed upside. One public accession, PI 88788, supplies SCN resistance in about 90-97% of resistant US soybean varieties (Iowa State: all but 29 of more than 1,000). So a single overlooked accession can carry an industry-wide trait. [Iowa State 2017](https://crops.extension.iastate.edu/cropnews/2017/11/over-1000-scn-resistant-soybean-varieties-all-29-have-pi-88788-resistance), [Purdue 2007](https://www.purdue.edu/uns/x/2007b/071114FaghihiSCN.html)
- S1 and S5: the same example shows the problem. Nobody owned PI 88788's value; it spread to every breeder. NPGS distributes to bona fide research entities, including commercial ones, in small lots (often 25 seeds, with caps on accessions per request), and some accessions are restricted. Access is cheap but non-exclusive, so the edge has to come from your own trial outcomes and from protecting derived lines. [NPGS distribution policy](https://npgstest.ars-grin.gov/gringlobal/distribution), [USDA Geneva requests](https://ars.usda.gov/northeast-area/geneva-ny/plant-genetic-resources-unit-pgru/docs/germplasm-requests)
