### 3. Powered brownfields: 18 -> 16 (fails)
- **The policy tailwind is real.** EPA's Jan 2026 guidance on redeveloping Superfund and brownfield sites as AI data centres followed EO 14318 ([EPA](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2026-01/guidance-on-the-redevelopment-of-superfund-and-brownfield-sites-as-ai-data-centers.pdf); [ABA](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/environment_energy_resources/resources/newsletters/superfund/epa-guidance-development-ai-data-centers-superfund-brownfield-sites/)). But CERCLA liability remains, and BFPP protection needs continuing obligations ([Bracewell](https://www.bracewell.com/resources/bringing-data-centers-to-brownfields/); [Greenberg Glusker](https://www.greenbergglusker.com/publications/understanding-cercla-liability-what-property-owners-need-to-know-before-buying-a-brownfield)).
- **S1 4 -> 3:** sellers look for a buyer that can absorb "boomerang" liability, and full-transfer deals rely on remediation trusts, guaranties and PLL insurance. Specialist liability buyers already exist: ELT says it has acquired $1.5B of liabilities, and Hilco Redevelopment buys retired coal plants for industrial and data-centre reuse ([Risk & Insurance](https://riskandinsurance.com/transferring-polluted-properties/); [DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/data-center-planned-old-chicago-power-station/)). The stigma discount is real, but specialists already bid it away on the sites with power.
- **S4 3 -> 2:** remediation, collateral and capital dominate, and an Ankura case shows how heavy the diligence is ([Ankura](https://ankura.com/insights/case-study-brownfield-to-data-center-technical-due-diligence-to-de-risk-300-mw-of-capacity)). **S2 4** holds, since powered land is worth far more than stigma land. **S5 4** holds: own cleanup outcomes compound.
