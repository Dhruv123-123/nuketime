## The law round 1 surfaced
Terranox works because four things hold at once:
1. **The claim is cheap and first-come.** A mineral claim costs a few dollars a hectare and the first
   filer gets exclusive rights, no auction, no incumbent's permission.
2. **The data is public but illegible.** Decades of scanned, messy filings that need ML to turn into a
   prediction; reading them better is a real edge even though everyone can download them.
3. **The asset is worth orders of magnitude more than the claim.**
4. **Realising the value does not need an incumbent's channel** (a discovery can be sold or developed).

Most non-geological scarce rights fail (1): they are auctioned (spectrum, aquaculture), need large
capital and site control (grid queues), go to qualified operators only (pharmacy, broadcast), or are
owned by funds (water, land). Round 2 hunts specifically for regimes where (1) holds outside geology,
then checks (2)-(4).
