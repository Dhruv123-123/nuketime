# Eighth hunt brief: public data + ML used to find and OWN the valuable thing

Written 15 October 2026 for every sweep agent.

## What Dhruv wants (his words, 15 Oct 2026)
"this is too small in that it's limited by the sheer size of the industry built around this one service
that we would provide. the thing is that datasets in anything can be used for anything. think of all the
datasets in the world that are public. dont bottleneck yourself. the thing which terranox does is uses
this data to find uranium. find something like this using public datasets that is unattackable"

## The template (Terranox)
Terranox uses SMAD (public Saskatchewan assessment filings) plus ML to predict where uranium is, then
stakes the ground. It does not sell analytics to miners; it owns the discovery. Its ceiling is the
value of what it finds, not the size of a software or service market. Its defence is that mineral
claims are exclusive: whoever reads the data best and claims first owns the asset.

So a candidate must have all of:
1. **Public dataset(s)** anywhere in the world, any domain (law-forced, scientific archive, government
   open data, satellite, registry, anything). Name it and say why it is public.
2. **ML does something hard on it** that humans and incumbents are not doing well: an inference
   problem (sparse, messy, multi-modal), not a lookup.
3. **The company owns the outcome**: it finds, claims, buys, builds, patents, makes or licenses-in the
   valuable thing itself. Ideally the thing is **rivalrous/claimable** (first to claim gets exclusive
   rights: a claim, permit, lease, queue position, option, patent, licence, exclusive licence), which
   is what makes it "unattackable".
4. **Ceiling set by the asset's value**, credibly multi-billion, not by a tool/analytics/service
   market. Anything whose ceiling is a $100-400M/yr software or service market is out.
5. **Not prospecting, exploration or drilling** for minerals, oil, gas, geothermal, hydrogen, brine,
   or pore space. Dhruv banned these (2026-10-03). Finding value in non-geological things is fine.

## Rules for agents
- Web search budget: at most 20 WebSearch calls for you (the whole run shares about 200). Spend them on
  prior-art and crowding checks, not on general background you already know.
- The cloud network blocks many government and data hosts. Verify dataset access by documentation; say
  plainly when you could not fetch something. Do not try to download big datasets.
- Do not call any `mcp__hearthbot__` tool. Do not commit or push.
- Be adversarial with yourself: for each candidate name who already does it (startups, incumbents,
  funds), and kill it if someone credible already owns the position.
