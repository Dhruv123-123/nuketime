# Round 4 brief (15 Oct 2026): Dhruv's correction to the test

Dhruv, 04:56Z, on the four-part Terranox test in PICK8: "These are untrue in that it's not necessarily
first come first serve you have to buy the mineral right ... but yes otherwise yes. the scope of the
problem needs to be such that machine learning and ai is what makes the problem (pain point) solvable".

## Corrected test
1. **You buy the right or asset** (no first-come claim needed) at a price set by sellers and rival buyers
   who cannot see its true value.
2. **The value is hidden in public data that is illegible**, and **ML/AI is what makes it solvable**: without
   ML the valuation cannot be done at scale, not merely done slower. A lookup or a spreadsheet join fails this.
3. **The asset is worth far more than its price** once the hidden value is known.
4. **The company realises the value itself** (develops, operates, sells the output), not by selling analytics.

Sectors: largely energy, machines, robotics, deeptech, security tech (Dhruv, 04:31Z). Still banned:
prospecting, exploration, drilling.

What changes: rounds 1-3 killed several ideas mainly because the right had to be bought, sold by tender,
or had an owner. Under the corrected test, buying is fine. What still kills an idea: ML is not what
unlocks the value; the mispricing is already visible to funds or strategics; the pool is small; or the
value cannot be realised without an incumbent's channel.
