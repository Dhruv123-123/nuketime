- 12 lanes, ~90 candidates, ~50 legal claim regimes. Nothing outside mineral tenure had all four of:
  cheap first-come claim; public but illegible data needing ML; asset worth far more than the claim;
  value realisable without an incumbent's permission.
- Killed in Dhruv's sectors already: powered land / grid queue positions (Cloverleaf 7 GW sold, Tract,
  GridCARE, Lancium; site control + deposits not first-come; our own backtest on Berkeley Lab Queued Up
  end-2024 -> end-2025 found an ML model AUC 0.72 vs a type x phase lookup 0.73, i.e. public queue data
  alone gives no edge); FERC hydro permits; retiring-plant interconnection rights (owners hold them);
  FAA PMA parts (non-exclusive, HEICO/TransDigm); nuclear obsolescence parts (Curtiss-Wright etc.);
  DLA alternate sourcing (non-exclusive); satellite spectrum filings (3/10); AI materials discovery
  (Periodic Labs, Lila, Radical AI, CuspAI, Orbital); hazardous-waste reclaimers (5/10).
- Pattern: where the claim is exclusive, the value is usually already priced by funds or strategics;
  where data is illegible, the claim is usually non-exclusive. Look for the exceptions.
