## 2. What killed/delayed projects
- NuScale/UAMPS CFPP (Nov 2023): $55→$89/MWh with ~$30 IRA credit; $5.3B→$9.3B for 462 MWe ≈ $20,139/kW. https://www.catf.us/2023/11/lessons-learned-recently-cancelled-nuscale-uamps-project/
- Vogtle 3&4: >$35B vs $14B, 7 yrs late; DOE cites incomplete design at construction start, unrealistic schedule, worker shortage, rework.
- Darlington: C$25,700/kW first unit; C$17,400/kW fleet avg; 4th unit ~C$13,700/kW.
- Lessons (NSI Mar 2026; CATF; Lazard): design completeness before concrete; 10–30 unit order books for 25–30% material cost reductions; fixed-price EPC with guarantees; secure HALEU before construction. https://www.ans.org/news/article-7870/nsi-report-addresses-supply-chain-bottlenecks/

## 8. Cost
- Lazard v19 (July 2026) unsubsidized: nuclear $175–255/MWh (capex $12,300–17,570/kW); CCGT $51–129 (capex $1,450–2,100; post-2028 quotes $2,400–2,600/kW); peaker $144–276; solar $40–98; 4-h LCOS $210–292. Nuclear LCOE up ~20% YoY. https://www.lazard.com/media/sdvdrvc5/lazards-lcoeplus_vf.pdf
- Real: Darlington ~C$149/MWh (≈US$108); CFPP $89/MWh subsidized at $20,139/kW; Vogtle ≈ $16,000/kW. FOAK SMR $80–180/MWh.
- No public SMR contracted prices. Assume FOAK SMR power 2–3x new CCGT.

## Observed gaps
1. HALEU deconversion and >5% UF6 transport packaging.
2. NQA-1/N-stamp qualification-as-a-service for ISO 9001 shops.
3. Second-source safety-related valves and small-bore pumps.
4. Nuclear NDE/inspection capacity and certified welders (mobile, NRC-recognized).
5. Uprate engineering bandwidth (16 applications in 2027).
6. Licensing documentation / QA-record tooling for Part 53/57 applicants.
7. Nuclear-grade graphite, Li-7, FLiBe.
8. Independent TRISO fabrication for microreactors.
9. Restart-specific outage services (SG tubes, decon, fuel handling).
10. Long-lead BOP brokerage/inventory (GSUs 18–24 mo).
11. Host-site and state-incentive development (ESP-ready nuclear sites).
12. FOAK completion-guarantee / insurance structuring for 50–300 MWe.

Weakest evidence: Valar mega-rounds (secondary), Army $2.2B (single source), SMR $/MWh beyond Darlington/CFPP.
