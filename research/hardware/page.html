Heat Sensor Kit

What hardware should the new company build itself? A field kit that collects heat data only we own, carried by the same team that signs the heat rights.

18 / 25

Build the sensor as the heat-rights bank's moat, not as a separate company. The kit makes our data private and our first screen about 100x cheaper per basin. It does not raise what a buyer pays before a power contract, so it does not lift the bank above its 20/25. Nothing outside geothermal beat it: the grid-headroom sensor play fell from 21 to 15 under red team.

The kit

Product 1 · now

Ranch water-well drop probe

A 16 mm probe that fits the pump tube logs temperature, pressure and conductivity in private wells. The rancher gets a free well check; we sign a geothermal option at about $1/acre/yr on the same visit.

Prototype $10-15k · 6-10 weeks

Product 2 · now

No-permit open-land screen

Logging 2 m temperature probes plus electric-field MT nodes. BLM IM 2026-024 makes them casual use until Sep 2029, and data from unleased land never has to be filed.

$2-8k per km² · one gradient hole buys a basin

Later rung

Own gradient rig + EGS rating

A light 500 m rig with fibre temperature sensing, then thermistor strings plus seismic nodes to rate EGS blocks before auction. Utah EGS land already clears at up to $1,207/acre.

Rig $1.1-1.5M · $60-120k per hole

Why it helps, and where it doesn't

  • Copyable data: fixed. Ranch-well profiles and year-long probe series are in no public database. State well logs have depth and water level, no temperature.
  • Cheap right: improved. Private ranch heat leased for about $2/acre against about $40/acre at BLM auction (Raser). Private land also sits outside the 51,200-acre federal cap.
  • Expensive proof: partly fixed. Owning a rig is a 2-5x gain, not the 12x first claimed.
  • Weak step-up: not fixed. Buyers re-drill before committing capital, so cheap proof lowers our cost, not their price.

Cheapest first test

30 days, about $25-40k in all.

  • Ranch wells ($8-15k, 21 days). Log 15+ wells in a valley with a known system and a control valley. Pass: 60%+ logged without pulling pumps, anomaly only in the known valley, 3+ ranchers sign $1/acre options.
  • Recall check (about $15k, 90 days logging). 40 logging probes over a known blind system and a producing field. Pass: anomaly 3x background, noise under 0.3 °C.
  • EGS desk test ($0-20k, 2-3 weeks). On public FORGE data, shallow holes predict 3 km temperature within 25 °C and surface seismic predicts stress direction within 20°.

Kill conditions

  • More than half of known fields show no shallow or ranch-well anomaly.
  • Ranch wells can't be logged without pulling pumps, or ranchers won't sign alongside the free check.
  • BLM field offices rule that probes left out for weeks are not casual use.
  • No developer will price an anomaly-stage package, even with our private data.
  • Zanskar or Ormat starts a private-land programme before we lock 15k+ acres.
