# g06: ASME BPVC / NBIC repair and alteration and R-stamp, plus API 510/570/653 owner-user repairs: pathway, time, cost, what is undone

Method: 3 WebSearch calls (quota), 4 WebFetch calls. Anything not from a fetched/search source is marked (recalled) and should be treated as an estimate from memory.

## 1. Evidence gathered (sourced)

- National Board R Certificate of Authorization requirements (fetched https://www.nationalboard.org/Index.aspx?pageID=115&ID=160):
  - Must hold an inspection agreement with an Authorized Inspection Agency (AIA); first page of AIA contract submitted with form NB-12.
  - Written quality system meeting current NBIC + NB-415 + scope; QC manual must show AIA acceptance.
  - On-site quality system review before issue (joint with ASME review or standalone; by a jurisdiction or NB representative).
  - Fees: $1,150 per three-year certificate; $150 steel stamp loan; $575 withdrawal; $50 revision; review fees/deposit extra.
  - Must hold current NBIC (all parts) and the applicable construction code.
- National Board site: the NBIC "is utilized by more than 5,200 repair organizations in over 60 countries" and underpins four accreditation programs: R (repair/alteration), VR (pressure relief valve repair), NR (nuclear repair), T/O (testing organizations). (https://www.nationalboard.org/Index.aspx?pageID=4)
- NBIC committee material (search snippets of nationalboard.org agendas/minutes):
  - NBIC Part 3 3.3.2 e) lists a closed set of "routine repairs" (e.g. non-load-bearing attachments not requiring PWHT) where jurisdictions may waive inspector involvement / R stamping.
  - 2023 Subgroup R&A item proposed new 5.2.2 d)/e) guidance for R holders with a DESIGN-ONLY scope and how they complete the R-2 form. So "design-only R holder" (an engineering house that does no welding) is a recognized and growing category.
  - 2022 proposal: R holder QC manual to reference NB-415.
  - Interpretation 07-16: R holder must prepare a detailed repair/alteration plan before work on Section VIII Div 2/3 vessels.
- Forum evidence (AWS forum, eng-tips; opinion, not authority): either API 510 or NBIC may govern weld repairs/alterations depending on jurisdiction and industry; software-assisted calcs must still be accepted by the Inspector; changes in dimension/contour are alterations not repairs.
- ASME course IPPD441 outline covers NBIC vs API 510 differences and FFS as part of the same repair framework (https://www.asme.org/getmedia/dc9d87ec-4bd2-439b-803d-ef39243bd382/ippd441_insprepair_desc-outline_1.pdf).

## 2. The pathway (two parallel regimes)

### A. NBIC Part 3 (jurisdictional; boilers and pressure vessels in commercial/industrial service, most US states and Canadian provinces)

| # | Step | Who | Time (recalled unless noted) | Cost (recalled) | Undone / broken |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Damage found at jurisdictional in-service inspection (boilers ~annual, PV 1-5 yr per state) or by owner | Jurisdiction inspector or AIA commissioned inspector (NB "IS" commission) | event-driven | inspection fee $100-500 per object | Findings are paper/PDF; no structured national damage dataset |
| 2 | Classify: routine repair / repair / alteration (re-rate, change of contour, nozzle add) | R holder + Inspector | hours-days | small | Classification is judgment; NBIC examples "not inclusive" (sourced) -> inconsistent between inspectors and states |
| 3 | Retrieve original design basis: Manufacturer's Data Report (U-1/U-1A, P-2), NB registration number, drawings | Owner, NB (if NB-registered), OEM | days-months; often impossible | $0-$5k | ORPHANED DATA: many old vessels have no MDR, OEM gone; NB holds copies only for NB-registered items; owner must reconstruct by measurement and conservative assumption |
| 4 | Engineering: code calcs to original construction code (ASME VIII-1 UG-27 shell, UG-37 nozzle reinforcement, MAWP), or FFS per API 579-1/ASME FFS-1 Level 1/2/3 to justify running as-is or minimal repair | R holder with design scope, often PE; FFS specialist firm | L1/2: 1-5 days; L3 (FEA, crack growth, creep): 2-8 weeks | L1/2 $3-20k; L3 $30-150k+ | Highly manual; heavy reuse of templated spreadsheets/COMPRESS; Level 3 throttled by a few hundred senior FFS engineers |

Accreditation layer: R certificate $1,150/3 yr + AIA contract + QC manual + review (sourced). PERMISSION IS CHEAP AND ABUNDANT here (5,200+ NBIC repair orgs). The scarce permission is the commissioned Inspector's signature and the AIA (AIAs are mostly insurers: HSB/Munich Re, FM Global, Zurich, Chubb, Allianz, Travelers, plus a few independents like Arise, OneCIS (recalled)). AIAs are themselves accredited by NB (NB-369) and ASME (QAI-1) (recalled).

### B. API owner-user regime (refining, petrochemical, chemicals)

- API 510 (pressure vessels), API 570 (piping), API 653 (atmospheric storage tanks). Jurisdictions accept these via Owner-User Inspection Organization (NB-371 "OUIO") or state law; many refinery states let owner-users self-inspect (recalled).
- Repair organization: owner-user, ASME/R stamp holder, or contractor acceptable to owner; work authorized by the API-certified inspector (API 510/570/653 individual certification exams) and, for alterations/many repairs, approved by a "pressure vessel engineer"/"piping engineer"/"storage tank engineer" (recalled from API 510 sec. 8, API 653 sec. 12).
- Inspection intervals: API 510 internal inspection at the lesser of 10 years or half remaining life, unless RBI (API 580/581) justifies otherwise (recalled). Thickness Measurement Locations (TMLs) feed corrosion-rate/remaining-life calcs in IDMS software.
- API 653: hydrotest after major repair can be waived on engineering evaluation (sec. 12.3) and FFS of bottoms/shell common (recalled).
- Key clock: TURNAROUNDS. Refinery units open every 4-6 years for 3-8 weeks; each day of overrun costs roughly $0.5-5M (recalled). "Discovery work" (unexpected damage) forces FFS/repair decisions in 24-72 hours, so FFS engineers are flown in and work nights. This is where speed of verification is worth the most.

## 4. What is undone and what would need to be done (assume abundant intelligence/compute)

1. Orphan-vessel reconstitution: generate a defensible "reconstructed Manufacturer's Data Report" from nameplate photos, laser scan, UT thickness grid, PMI (XRF/OES) and era-specific code editions, with explicit conservatism, so an R holder can re-rate/alter with Inspector acceptance. Needs: an NBIC interpretation or new Part 3 paragraph defining minimum evidence for reconstructed design data. Nobody does this systematically.
2. Approval-grade calc + FFS engine operated BY a design-only R holder with PEs: Level 1/2 in minutes, Level 3 FEA/crack-growth/creep in hours (automated meshing, verification against analytic benchmarks, sensitivity sweeps), output as a clause-by-clause compliance matrix the Inspector can check. KPI: engineer hours and Inspector hours per accepted R-2/FFS. The NBIC design-only R holder category (2023 R&A item) is exactly the legal vehicle.
3. Machine-checkable acceptance: a structured, digitally signed R-1/R-2 package (calc, WPS/PQR link, NDE results, test record) with automated pre-check against NBIC/ASME/API text, so the Inspector reviews exceptions only. Needs NB/jurisdiction acceptance of electronic data reports and e-signature (partially exists; status uncertain (recalled)).
4. PQR economics: a shared, very large PQR library (and a lab running coupons at volume, with AI choosing essential-variable combinations to maximize coverage per coupon) licensed to R holders. Legal constraint: ASME IX requires the organization to take responsibility for its own PQRs; AWS SWPS (QW-500) is the precedent for standardized procedures. Would need expanded SWPS coverage or a code case.
5. AI NDE interpretation accepted by code: ASME Section V and API currently assume a qualified human (ASNT Level II/III) interprets. Path: performance-demonstration qualification of an algorithm (like PDI for nuclear UT, Section XI App VIII) via code case. Physics clock: generating flawed specimens and blind trials (months).
6. Quantified hydrotest-waiver standard: probabilistic justification (NDE POD + FFS margin) replacing inspector comfort; NBIC/API proposal.
7. Remote/witnessed-by-video hold points with AI verification of identity, location, procedure parameters (amperage, preheat, PWHT chart) to cut Inspector travel; needs jurisdiction acceptance.
8. AI-native AIA: become an accredited Authorized Inspection Agency (NB-369/ASME QAI-1) whose commissioned inspectors each cover 5-10x the volume with AI pre-review. This attacks the actually scarce permission. Constraints: AIAs historically insurers (liability and capital), commissioned inspectors need NB exams and experience (recalled).
9. Turnaround "discovery desk": 24/7 FFS + repair-design + WPS + plan delivered within hours of a finding, priced against $/day of outage.
10. National damage/repair dataset: R-1/R-2 reports and owner-user IDMS data are siloed; a pooled, anonymized dataset (damage mechanism by API 571, material, service) would let FFS and RBI be calibrated. Data-access constraint: owner confidentiality; would need insurer or NB partnership.

## 5. Constraints and what would change them
- Physics clocks: PQR coupons (1-4 wks), hydro tests (days + outage), PWHT (hours-days), NDE algorithm qualification trials (months). Change: pre-qualify at scale; code cases for alternatives.
- Law/regulator acceptance: state boiler laws adopt NBIC by reference; NBIC editions every 2 years (2023, 2025, next 2027) (recalled); anyone may submit proposals to NB committees. So rule change is slow but open.
- Liability: PE stamp and Inspector signature carry liability; AIAs are insurers. An AI house needs E&O insurance and its own PEs.
- Data access: original MDRs, owner IDMS data, damage histories.

## 6. Opinion: fit with the Approval Factory
- Unlike DLA SAR / FAA PMA, the "permission" here (R stamp) is cheap ($1,150/3 yr, sourced) and widely held (5,200+ orgs). The moat is not the certificate; it is engineering hours (FFS L3), Inspector hours, PQR inventory and turnaround speed. So this sector is a VERIFICATION-THROUGHPUT play, not a permission-scarcity play.
- Best AI wedge: a design-only R holder + FFS engineering firm that is AI-native, selling to refiners/chemical plants on turnaround speed, later moving up to become an AIA. Incumbent revenue pool is services-heavy (E2G, Quest/Team, Mistras, SES, Becht), meaning a services-margin business that AI could convert to software-like margins.
- Relevance to defense legacy platforms: weaker; aviation/defense parts approvals use different regimes. Could be a second product line for the same "verification engine" (code-compliance checking + FEA automation + compliance matrix), since the core capability (generate reviewer-ready evidence packages) transfers.
