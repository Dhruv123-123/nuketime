# Construction Side of Nuclear, Data Center, and Energy Infrastructure (2026-09-25)

## 1. Why nuclear construction goes over budget
- Vogtle 3&4: ~$31B (2024$, ex-financing) / ~$35B incl. Westinghouse bankruptcy payments / "$36B" popular figure; original ~$14B; ~25% Georgia Power residential rate increase. Use ~$15,000/kW OCC (2024$) as comparable. https://sai.inl.gov/content/uploads/29/2025/06/M3_SAI-AP1000_Lessons_Rev6-nocomments-002.pdf ; https://www.utilitydive.com/news/after-2-years-ratepayer-pain-political-fallout-georgia-nuclear-vogtle/817792/
- DOE Liftoff (Sept 2024) decomposition of ~$15,000/kW: ~$3,800/kW (25%) Vogtle-specific inefficiencies (incomplete design, Part 52 with incomplete design, designer bankruptcy); ~$2,900/kW (20%) true FOAK; ~$8,300/kW (55%) next two-unit basis; four-unit program ~$7,500/kW; after ITC ~$4,500–5,000/kW. Root-cause chain: incomplete design → rework, limited constructability review, module supply-chain issues, inadequate integrated schedule/project controls, inadequate QA/QC, low productivity, attrition, labor shortage. FOAK→NOAK levers (indexed): rework elimination ~65, design standardization ~35, workforce experience ~35, bulk ordering ~15. Unit 4 ~20% cheaper than Unit 3. Committed orderbook of 5–10 deployments is step one; ±20% on-budget is credibility threshold. https://gain.inl.gov/content/uploads/4/2024/11/DOE-Advanced-Nuclear-Liftoff-Report.pdf
- INL AP1000 lessons (Apr 30, 2025): 180+ LARs against DCD Rev 19; projected 2nd/3rd/4th two-unit plants $10,000/$7,800/$6,200 per kWe in 7/6/5.5 yrs; NOAK ~$4,700/kWe, 50 months. China FOAK AP1000s ~8–9 yrs; CAP1000 second-series ~4.5–5 yrs; Sanmen 1 ~$2,440/kW. Nine lessons: finalize design; constructability review; real integrated schedule; contractor QA (NRC found inadequate QA at module facility); risk assessment; early workforce training; finance for FOAK shocks; single accountable EPC; align licensing with design maturity.
- MIT Joule 2020: indirect costs explain 72% of 1976–87 increase; home-office engineering, field supervision, temporary facilities, payroll insurance. https://www.cell.com/joule/fulltext/S2542-4351(20)30458-X
- Hinkley Point C: £35bn (2015£) ≈ £48.7bn nominal vs £18bn; Unit 1 to 2030; M&E installation productivity is the binding constraint. https://www.newcivilengineer.com/latest/hinkley-point-cs-cost-climbs-to-35bn-with-confirmation-unit-1-will-power-up-in-2030-20-02-2026/
- Flamanville 3: €3.3bn → €13.2bn; 2007 → full power Dec 2025.
- Barakah: ~97–104 months first-concrete-to-grid per unit, ~$20.4B contract. Hualong One target 60 months; Fangchenggang 3 ~85 months; target $2,800–3,500/kW.
- Bottom line: design incompleteness at first concrete, rework and module QA failures, thin integrated schedules, craft shortage/productivity, supply-chain/module delivery.

## 3. Workforce
- ~439,000-worker construction shortage, mostly electricians/pipe layers; DC construction pays ~30% above typical; DC-area IBEW local doubled 7,350 (2018) → 14,700 (2025). https://itif.org/publications/2026/01/12/construction-industry-facing-worker-shortage-driven-by-growth-of-data-centers/
- ABC: 349,000 net-new needed 2026, 456,000 in 2027. AGC/NCCER: 92% of firms have difficulty hiring. Electrical ≈ half of DC labor. Craft wages +20% since COVID.
- Wages: BLS median electrician $63,190; DC premium 15–30%; journeyman all-in $120–190K in hot markets; staffing markups 25–75%. https://www.rinvio.com/blog/data-center-electrician-crew-cost-benchmark
- Nuclear: 67,900 workers 2024; 63% of nuclear manufacturers say hiring very difficult. https://www.energy.gov/ne/articles/3-workforce-trends-nuclear-energy-2025 ; NCEA: skilled-trades FTE demand 8,200–9,100 (2025) → 16,000–21,500 (2030). https://energyanalytics.org/research/nuclear-workforce ; Vogtle trained ~30,000 workers. Unverified: "<5,000 nuclear-certified welders", "50,000 craft shortfall by 2030."
- Platforms: Skillit (generic craft marketplace); The Nuclear Company NOS. No dedicated nuclear-craft credentialing marketplace found.

## 4. Supply chain and QA
- N-stamp: Sandia 2021 study; no current public dataset of N/NPT/NA/MO or NQA-1 holders — a gap. https://www.sandia.gov/research/publications/details/global-trends-of-asme-n-stamp-certifications-for-nuclear-component-vendors-2021-08-17/
- NRC RIC 2026 "Critical Links": obstacles = limited global manufacturing capacity for nuclear-grade components, evolving QA requirements, need for new supplier qualification pathways. https://www.nrc.gov/public-involve/conference-symposia/ric/past/2026/technical-sessions/sessionabstract6
- CFSI: NRC IG (Feb 2022): DOE >100 counterfeit/fraudulent/suspect instances in one year; industry databases reported <10 since 2016; NRC has no CFSI collection process. NEA CFSI workshop Oct 7–8, 2026.
- DOE $17.5B LLI conditional commitment (June 30, 2026): 5 SPV loans; each utility and Westinghouse post $500M escrow; 7 utilities LOIs for 5 slots; pull schedules forward up to 3 years. https://www.neimagazine.com/news/doe-backs-ap1000-reactor-build-out/
- Startups: Forged Operations (supplier-qualification SaaS), thinly documented.

## 8. Financing and contracting
- Westinghouse: fixed-price for long-lead components with LDs but "does not provide construction services or assume any construction risk." No major US EPC has taken lump-sum turnkey on a new reactor since V.C. Summer/Vogtle.
- Darlington: C$20.9B / 4 units ≈ C$17.4M/MW; IPD alliance (OPG, GEH, AtkinsRéalis, Aecon); Canada Growth Fund C$2B + Building Ontario Fund C$1B equity. https://www.worldnuclearreport.org/Ontario-s-Darlington-SMR-project-to-cost-nearly-21-billion-significantly-higher
- Sizewell C: £38bn RAB; UK 44.9%, La Caisse 20%, Centrica 15%, EDF 12.5%, Amber 7.6%; equity+debt sized above cost.
- Liftoff risk instruments: completion/debt guarantees, contingent equity/debt, overrun insurance; 48E ITC is a de facto partial overrun hedge. No commercial construction-overrun insurance for nuclear found.
- V.C. Summer: Brookfield/The Nuclear Company JV (May 2026); feasibility phase 1 done July 2026.

## Observed gaps
1. NQA-1 supplier qualification + CGD as shared service (modern NUPIC with digital evidence packages).
2. Live registry of N-stamp/NPT/MO/NQA-1 holders.
3. Module-fabrication QA software (weld traceability, welder identity, digital travelers, NCR linkage).
4. Nuclear craft credentialing and mobility platform.
5. Constructability / design-completeness scoring before first concrete.
6. Nuclear-grade integrated schedule and risk analytics (ITAAC linkage, inspection windows).
7. Construction-overrun risk capital/insurance for nuclear.
8. Electrical-gear allocation, secondary market, prefab MV/LV substations.
9. Gas-turbine slot reservation finance and swaps; EPC cost benchmarking.
10. PSD/Title V air-permit and NRC Part 51 ER automation.
11. Advanced-manufacturing qualification services (PM-HIP, EBW, AM) to ASME III/NRC.
12. Long-lead-item program controls for the $17.5B SPV structure.
