# Drill hole cost hunt: brief for every research agent

Written 19 October 2026.

## The ask (Dhruv, 19 Oct 2026)
"look into the other painpoints of the terranox pipeline ... insanely expensive exploratory drill holes".
This hunt covers ONLY exploratory drill holes. Helicopter/airborne surveys belong to another thread;
do not research them except where they are part of drill-program support (e.g. heli-supported rigs).

## Context
Terranox (Dhruv's company, YC W26, already built) uses the public Saskatchewan Mineral Assessment
Database (SMAD) plus ML to find uranium in the Athabasca Basin. It buys mineral rights, then proves them
with airborne surveys and exploratory drill holes. A drill hit takes land from ~$1/ha to ~$10,000/ha.
Holes are very expensive and usually miss.

The question: is there a NEW company (not a Terranox feature, not Terranox itself) in making exploratory
holes cheaper, fewer, or more informative? Feasibility is the deliverable, not implementation.

## Dhruv's gold rubric (how ideas are scored)
1. Within some discrete, sellable rung of a vertical-integration ladder, the hard, expensive,
   usually-failing step ("discovery") is 80%+ of the work and the physical step is trivial/abstracted.
2. Signal sits in public-but-illegible data that incumbents do not extract; ML/software is what makes
   the pain point solvable at all, not a nice-to-have.
3. The unproven right/asset can be bought cheap.
4. A capitalised buyer pays a step-up for the proven state at once.
5. A ladder to climb: win the smallest rung, step up toward doing the whole chain.
6. Ceiling: credibly a very large company, not a $100-400M/yr tool or service market.
Base hit rates of 30-70% are now accepted (lever 1.5-3x), not only 10x.

Earlier rule: Dhruv banned prospecting/drilling-based picks on 2026-10-03; he has now asked for this
pain point explicitly. Do not filter ideas for that rule; just note where an idea sits relative to it.

## Rules for agents
- Use WebSearch/WebFetch for evidence. Cite a URL for every number. Mark anything from memory as
  "(memory)" and any estimate as "(estimate)". Prefer primary sources: NI 43-101 / JORC technical
  reports, company MD&A and financial statements, contractor annual reports, government statistics,
  S&P Global / MinEx Consulting figures, academic papers.
- Currency: say CAD or USD.
- Be adversarial: for each idea, name who already does it and how well it went (funding, failure,
  traction). Kill what is already owned.
- Do not call any `mcp__hearthbot__` tool. Do not commit or push. Write only the one file you are told.
- Keep your file under ~600 lines; lead with a 10-line summary of the findings that matter most.
