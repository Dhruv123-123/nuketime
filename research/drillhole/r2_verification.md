# Round 2: checking the numbers the verdict rests on

Written 19 Oct 2026. CAD unless marked USD. "(estimate)" = my arithmetic. "(memory)" = not re-verified.
Status labels: **CONFIRMED** = matches a primary source; **CORRECTED** = the round-1 figure was wrong or needs qualifying; **UNVERIFIED** = no source found.

## 10-line summary
1. **Exploration spend (NRCan, April 2026): CONFIRMED.** Canada C$4.4B in 2025 (preliminary) and C$5.3B intended for 2026. Uranium C$458M (2025) and C$737M intended (2026). Saskatchewan, all commodities, C$711M (2025) and C$823M intended (2026).
2. **Saskatchewan government figures are on a different basis.** The province says C$483M for 2026 (+23%), against NRCan's C$823M. Quote NRCan and label the provincial number "exploration only (estimate)". Neither source gives Athabasca metres drilled. NRCan's national count is 4.6M m of surface diamond drilling in 2024.
3. **SMAD confidentiality: CORRECTED to a primary source.** Assessment work stays confidential until the earliest of 3 years from receipt, holder consent, or the claim lapsing (Mineral Tenure Registry Regulations s.68). The same regulations require "complete geological drill logs" and assays in each assessment report.
4. **SMAD size: newly measured.** The province's assessment-file map service holds **11,494 distinct off-confidential files** (all commodities, the whole province). About **4,436 mention drilling** (estimate, by keyword). Most files date from the 1950s-1980s.
5. **SMAD file format: partly confirmed.** The files are downloadable "digital copies… may include PDF files". That pre-1990s files are scanned images, not text, is an inference (estimate). I did not check a sample file.
6. **Athabasca per-metre rates (primary 43-101s).** C$300/m contractor rate for 600 m holes (SRK for UEX/Denison, Christie Lake, 2022). C$525/m all-in for shallow NW-basin holes (Global Uranium 43-101, 2024). C$400/m (CanAlaska, 2010). These are consistent with Lane A's IsoEnergy MD&A figures (C$303/m drilling line on about 400 m holes, C$504/m on about 900 m). **No 43-101 helicopter line item was found (UNVERIFIED).**
7. **MinEx/Schodde: CONFIRMED.** About 700,000 m of drilling and about US$200M per new Moderate-or-larger discovery, up from about 200,000 m in 1985 (March 2019). Note: this is a global figure, not uranium-specific.
8. **Earth AI: claims stay UNVERIFIED.** The only third-party check is JORC reporting by its ASX partner Legacy Minerals on Fontenoy (34 m at 0.5 g/t PGE). That is a weak intercept, not a deposit. No project sale was found. Its option is to buy the Legacy tenements for US$1M plus a 2% royalty. Its US$100/m cost is still a company target.
9. **Durin and Ideon: no new rounds found.** Durin is still at a US$3.4M pre-seed (Apr 2025). Ideon's last equity was a US$16M Series A (Sep 2022), plus an undisclosed 2026 grant.
10. **Other checks.** Quebec C$110.52/m (2006) to C$170.16/m (2018) is CONFIRMED, but it covers all *off-mine-site* drilling, not "greenfield" specifically. Seequent at about US$1.05B is CONFIRMED (closed 17 Jun 2021). IMDEX revenue was A$431.4M in FY25 and A$520M in FY26 (CONFIRMED). Uranium is on the CMETC list (CONFIRMED).
