# Lane A: what an Athabasca exploratory drill hole costs today, and why

Written 19 Oct 2026. Currency is CAD unless marked USD. "(memory)" = from prior knowledge, not re-verified this session.
"(estimate)" = my arithmetic or judgement. Every other number has a URL.
Research note: the shared WebSearch budget ran out partway through. The best numbers came afterwards from primary
filings pulled directly from SEC EDGAR: IsoEnergy, Denison and NexGen 2025 annual MD&A and financial statements, and the Paladin ASX quarterlies.

## 10-line summary

1. **All-in cost is about C$450-750 per metre for a normal Athabasca diamond hole, and over C$1,000/m for a small, deep, remote program.** Across 14 programs I worked out $/m for, the median is about C$550/m (estimate).
2. **One hole costs about C$200k-550k**: a 400 m eastern-basin hole is about C$220k (IsoEnergy Larocque East, actual), a 750-780 m hole is about C$370k-545k (Denison Wheeler, NexGen PCE). A 4-hole deep program cost C$1.02M per hole (IsoEnergy Hawk, 2025 actual).
3. **The contractor's drilling line is only about 55% of the all-in cost.** IsoEnergy's audited 2025 cost breakdown for Larocque East (15,597 m, 39 holes) shows drilling C$303/m. Camp adds C$75/m, labour C$65/m, assays C$17/m, plus community, health/safety and travel, for C$549/m in all.
4. **Fixed season costs dominate small programs.** Camp, labour, geophysics to site holes, community and health/safety are roughly C$1-3M per project-season whatever the metres drilled. That is why 4 holes at Hawk cost about 2x per metre what 39 holes at Larocque did.
5. **Depth drives cost per hole, and the cost per metre rises too.** The drilling line alone was C$303/m on about 400 m holes and C$504/m on about 900 m holes (both IsoEnergy 2025). Holes are deep because the unconformity is deep: about 320 m at Hurricane, about 810-830 m at West McArthur, and NexGen PCE holes run 700-1,300 m.
6. **Year-round road access with an existing camp is the cheap end.** Paladin PLS ran about USD 267/m (about C$365/m) and Denison Wheeler about C$472/m. Helicopter-supported, fly-in programs are the expensive end.
7. **Basin-wide, discovery is very costly.** C$2.87B was spent from 1967-2017 for 75 "deposits" (C$38M each), but only 25 mined deposits (C$114M each). Phoenix took 31 years from first work and PLS Triple R took 36; Arrow took 2 (Wallis/CIM 2018).
8. **Greenfield holes mostly miss.** A typical junior season is 5-20 holes and 2,500-8,000 m, about C$2-5M. Standard Uranium's 2026 season returned 3 "anomalous" holes out of 12 and no mineralization. Step-out programs around an existing discovery hit 60-90%: NexGen 19/30, CanAlaska 13/22, Paladin Atlas 7/8.
9. **Drilling plus the costs that only exist to support drilling is about 70-80% of a junior's project spend.** The drilling line alone is 45-55% (IsoEnergy Canada: 44.5% of cash spend; Larocque East: 55%).
10. **Biggest cost levers, in order:** (a) fewer holes, through better targeting; (b) fewer metres per hole, through daughter/wedge holes and stopping holes early; (c) more metres per camp-season, to spread the fixed costs; (d) logistics, road versus helicopter and winter versus summer; (e) faster decisions from probe and assay turnaround. The contractor's $/m rate is a smaller lever (about ±15%).

**How to read the spread (estimate):**
- **About C$350-500/m:** road access, an existing camp, 700-800 m holes, large programs. Paladin PLS (by Hwy 955), Denison Wheeler (Phoenix site).
- **About C$500-650/m:** eastern-basin juniors with a seasonal camp and 300-450 m holes. IsoEnergy Larocque, Hook Lake, Murphy Lake.
- **About C$700-800/m:** deep holes with a heavy support load. NexGen PCE direct costs alone (camp and labour excluded) are already C$720-740/m on 740 m average holes, a few over 1,000-1,300 m.
- **About C$800-1,150/m:** small or deep or helicopter programs where fixed costs are spread over few metres. IsoEnergy Hawk; the IsoEnergy–Purepoint JV budget.
- **Inflation:** the same Hook Lake JV went from about $400/m in 2013 to $455-571/m in 2019-20 (budgets). Current actuals for comparable eastern-basin holes are about $520-550/m.

Big programs are about 30+ holes; small programs are about 4-10 holes. Deeper holes cost more per metre for three reasons:
- the drill pulls and runs a longer rod string for every core run;
- the hole size steps down (HQ to NQ) and bigger rigs are needed;
- the sandstone has clay-altered zones that need casing or cementing. Standard Uranium had to restart a hole "because of difficult ground" ([Newsfile](https://www.newsfilecorp.com/release/316802/)).

## 4. Fixed versus variable: a simple model

This is fitted to the Larocque and Hawk 2025 actuals (estimate).
- Cost per program-season ≈ **F + v × metres**, with F ≈ $1.0-1.5M per project-season and v ≈ $300-500/m depending on depth.
- **Larocque (2 seasons, 15.6k m):** 2 × $1.2M + 15,597 × $395/m (drilling + assays + variable labour) ≈ $8.6M. Matches.
- **Hawk (1 season, 3.6k m, deep):** $1.9M (including $0.9M geophysics) + 3,591 × $610/m ≈ $4.1M. Matches.
- **What it implies:** for a 5-hole, 2,500 m junior season the fixed share is about 45-50%, so all-in is about $900-1,000/m. For a 40-hole season it falls to about 25-30%. **Spreading fixed costs over more metres** is the largest cost lever after cutting holes.

## 5. Hit rates, program sizes, basin volume

**Discovery economics (Roger Wallis, CIM-MES 2018).** Source: [PDF](https://www.cimmes.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/Roger-Wallis_Athabasca-U-50-years-and-3-billion-dollars-later_31Oct2018.pdf).
- C$2.869B (money of the day) was spent in the Athabasca from 1967-2017.
- Number of deposits found depends on the definition:
  - 75 "deposits" by the loosest definition: C$38M each;
  - 61 when nearby zones are merged: C$47M each;
  - **25 actually mined: C$114M each**.
- By size: 62 deposits over 10 Mlb (C$46M each); 19 over 50 Mlb (C$150M each); 8 over 100 Mlb (C$360M each on average, though each one actually cost far less than C$100M).
- Average discovery cost is about C$1.02/lb overall, about C$0.49/lb for recent junior-operated discoveries.
- Discovery took 31 years at Phoenix, 36 years at PLS Triple R, 7 years at Key Lake and Cigar Lake, and 2 years at Arrow.
- Juniors and prospectors were operator on 20 of 121 deposits and significant intercepts, holding 518 Mlb.

**Implied holes per discovery (estimate):**
- At about C$550/m with drilling about 70-80% of spend, C$38M is about 50-55k m of drilling. At 400-800 m per hole that is **roughly 70-130 holes per "deposit"**.
- On the mined-deposit definition (C$114M) it is **about 200-400 holes per mined deposit**.
- Discovery is extremely skewed: most holes on most properties never hit anything.

**Recent hit rates. Greenfield and step-out drilling are very different.**

| Program | Type | Hits |
|---|---|---|
| Standard Uranium, Davidson River 2026 | Greenfield conductor tests | 3 of 12 holes "anomalous radioactivity" (>300 cps), no mineralization claimed ([Newsfile](https://www.newsfilecorp.com/release/316802/)) |
| IsoEnergy, Hawk 2025 | Greenfield, deep | 4 holes; MD&A reports re-interpretation and no headline intercept ([MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1997377/000110465926020794/isou-20251231xex99d2.htm)) |
| IsoEnergy, Larocque winter 2025 | Near-deposit + trend | 6 of 17 holes "strongly elevated radioactivity" (same) |
| Purepoint–IsoEnergy, Dorado 2025 | First JV program | Mineralization in 4 holes, giving the "Nova Discovery" (same) |
| NexGen, PCE 2024 | Post-discovery step-outs | 19 of 30 mineralized ([NXE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1698535/000127956924001366/ex991.htm)) |
| CanAlaska, West McArthur summer 2025 | Pike Zone step-outs | 13 of 22 mineralized ([Newsfile](https://www.newsfilecorp.com/release/273365/CanAlaska-Expands-West-McArthur-Pike-Zone-Footprint-To-500-Metres-Strike-Length)) |
| Paladin, Atlas 2026 | Discovery + follow-up | 7 of 8 holes ([PDN Jun-26](https://announcements.asx.com.au/asxpdf/20260722/pdf/071y23h50wdtpp.pdf)) |

Takeaway (estimate):
- On a *new property*, a "hit" (any mineralization) is perhaps 5-25% per hole and an economic discovery well under 1% per hole.
- Once a mineralized structure is found, step-outs hit 60-90%.
- So the expensive, usually-failing step is the **first 10-50 holes on a conductor trend**.

## 7. Share of a junior's spend that is drilling

- **Direct drilling line:** about 45-55% of project spend. IsoEnergy Larocque East 55%; IsoEnergy Canada total 45% (2025) and 36% (2024); Hawk 44%.
- **Drilling plus costs that exist only because rigs are turning** (camp, logging labour, assays, health/safety, community, travel): **about 75-85%** of project spend on a drilling project. Larocque: 55 + 13.7 + 11.8 + 3 + 3.2 + 3.3 + 2.6 = about 93% of cash, excluding share comp and geophysics.
- **At company level, including G&A, claim holding and target generation:** drilling-related spend is about **55-70% of a junior's total exploration budget** (estimate).

| 1 | **Number of holes**, through better targeting and kill decisions | Each avoided greenfield hole saves C$200k-550k (up to C$1M on deep remote ground), and holes per discovery are 70-400. This is where the money is, and it fits the "fewer or more informative holes" thesis. |
| 2 | **Metres per hole** | Depth is fixed by the unconformity. Cuts come from wedging/daughter holes from one parent (Denison's WR-837AD2 and WR-831D1D2 naming), stopping holes early on probe results, and placing collars better. A daughter hole skips the 300-800 m of sandstone above the branch point. |
| 3 | **Fixed-cost absorption** | Running more metres per camp-season and sharing camps or rigs between juniors or joint ventures takes small programs from C$900-1,100/m to C$500-600/m. |
| 4 | **Logistics** | Road versus helicopter versus fly-in; winter versus summer. Paladin and Denison show road access with an existing camp at C$365-470/m. |
| 5 | **Decision speed** | Fast gamma, XRF and hyperspectral logging at the rig instead of waiting weeks for SRC assays. Saves rig-days and the next mis-sited hole, not the assay budget itself (only 3%). |
| 6 | **Contractor rate** | About ±10-15%. The market is tight: Orbit Garant has 180 rigs at 70% utilisation and says it renegotiated prices on most contracts upward ([Orbit Garant FY2026](https://orbitgarant.com/nouvelles?a=122650)). |

**Relevance to the brief (estimate).** The dollars sit in *hole count* times *fixed season cost*, not in contractor rates.
- A new company would cut the most cost by reducing holes per discovery from about 100 to about 30-50, or by turning each miss into more information, for example by vectoring from alteration halos.
- Making the metre itself cheaper is mostly owned by large drilling contractors (Major Drilling, Orbit Garant, Foraco), and the ceiling there is about 15-30%.
- Sharing camps or logistics across juniors is a services play limited by the size of the basin: about C$200-300M per year of drilling-related spend (estimate). That is below Dhruv's ceiling test on its own.
