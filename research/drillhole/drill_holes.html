Drill hole cost hunt

Is there a new company in making exploratory holes cheaper, fewer or more informative? Fourteen research agents covered costs, the global market, rigs, sensing, business models, public data, operations and other industries that pay for expensive proving holes.

Verdict

No separate venture-scale company. A hole's cost is people, days on site and logistics, not rock physics. Contractors, Imdex and the targeting companies already hold every part that can be squeezed. The large lever, drilling fewer holes, only pays whoever owns the ground, which is what Terranox already does. The output is four Terranox features.

Cost per metre has not fallen in decades. Quebec's census went from C$110/m in 2006 to C$170/m in 2018, and Australia from A$89/m to A$207/m over 1997–2017. It follows the drilling cycle. The world now drills about 700,000 m per discovery, up from 200,000 m in 1985, so the waste is in how many holes it takes.

Why the pool is small

Contracted exploration and resource drilling is about USD 7.5B a year worldwide, and true discovery holes about USD 1.5–2B. Uranium is USD 0.35–0.6B. The biggest contractor-manufacturer, Boart Longyear, was taken private for about USD 371M in 2024. Imdex, the tool leader, takes about A$2.40 of every A$100 spent on exploration. Those are the sizes a drilling service or tool reaches.

Take into Terranox instead

  1. Sequential drill planner. Pick the next hole from what earlier holes found, and decide when to wedge or walk away. A Stanford/KoBold paper matched a grid's go/no-go accuracy with under half the holes in simulation. Each avoided hole saves C$200k–550k.
  2. Stopped-short hole list. 493 historic holes of 100 m or more have no recorded unconformity while holes within 2 km reach it deeper. Median length 192 m, median shortfall 84 m, about 120k m already drilled.
  3. Buy neighbours' misses. Pay juniors for unreleased logs on adjacent claims, partly in royalty, the way oil explorers have done for a century.
  4. Sell targets on ground Terranox doesn't want for 1–2% royalties, and build the hole-outcome base rates for its own stop-or-continue calls.
Watch: the 15% and 30% flow-through exploration credits lapse on 31 March 2027 unless extended. Uranium is on the 30% list, so that hits every junior's 2027 financing, Terranox's included.
